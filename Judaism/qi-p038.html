From among all nations or from Abraham's seed?

January 1, 2000
israel christianity judaism bible genesis theology #Beautified

In Surah 29:27 we read:

Some debate whether "his seed" refers to Jacob as the last person mentioned or to Abraham (him) whom Isaac and Jacob were given to. In either case, the prophethood is stated to belong to Abraham's descendents.

If all prophets are from Abraham's seed as Surah 29:27 declares, it can hardly be possible that at the same time there are messengers from among every people:

Does anybody intend to claim that all people groups are physical descendants of Abraham?

And there are further problems with the prophets from Abraham's progeny.
