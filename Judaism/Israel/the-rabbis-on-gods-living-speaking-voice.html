The Talmud contains an interesting story in respect to Exodus 19 and 20 when God descended upon Mount Sinai in order to allow the Israelites to hear his Voice audibly.

According to the rabbis, the Israelites died after hearing the Divine Voice which caused the Voice to complain to God!

This hypostatization of the Divine Speech, where God’s Voice is depicted as a personally distinct living, conscious Entity, comports with the Christian understanding of Jesus as the uncreated Word of God who is sent to reveal God to mankind:
