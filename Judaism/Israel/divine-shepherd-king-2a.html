The Solution

The Hebrew Bible provides the solution to this seemingly irreconcilable problem.

The Prophetic writings testify that a Child will be born to rule over David’s throne who shall be the Mighty God himself:

What makes this prophecy so shocking is that Isaiah applies the phrase “Mighty God” to Yahweh himself in the very next chapter!

In fact, this is title never applied in a positive sense to any other Being besides Yahweh:

As if this weren’t shocking enough, the Messiah is even called Yahweh our righteousness!

To top it off, the prophetic writings further proclaim that the Messiah comes from eternity itself:

Amazingly, the prophet has taken the very same terms which the Hebrew Bible uses to describe the eternal, uncreated nature of Yahweh,

And applied them to the coming Messianic Shepherd-King!

Hence, the solution to the dilemma is that Yahweh God himself will become a human descendant of David in order to fulfill all the promises made to both David and Israel that God’s people will have only one shepherd to rule over them forever and ever.

With that said it is time to unveil the identity of this God-Man, which we shall do in the next section.
