ISRAEL’S MULTI-PERSONAL GOD

November 23, 2021

The Hebrew Bible proclaims that Jehovah is the only Elohim (“God”) who reigns from heaven forever, and the only Adonay (“Lord”) that a believer has and trusts in:

The inspired Hebrews prophets also testified that there is no other Mighty God besides Jehovah, being the only God whom all nations must serve and worship:

And yet the God-breathed Scriptures of the Jews further identify another divine Person who reigns alongside of Jehovah in heaven,

Who is the Adonay1that the nations are required to serve and worship, since he is the eternally reigning Elohim,

And the Mighty God who steps out of eternity to enter into his creation in order to humble himself to be born as a human child in order to inherit David’s throne forever. The Holy Spirit filled seers announced that this male Child will come out of Bethlehem and shine forth from Galilee of the Gentiles:

And according to the NT writings, this eternal Being who chose to become human is none other than the Lord Jesus Christ!

Thus, in perfect harmony with the Christian NT documents the Hebrew Bible bears witness that the one true Lord God of all creation is a multi-Personal Being, and that one of those divine Persons shall become the human Messiah, whom both Christians and Muslims agree is none other than Jesus of Nazareth!
