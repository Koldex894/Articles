The Talmud’s Messiah

June 21, 2025
judaism messiah midrash prophecy #Beautified 2025

In this article I will quote certain sections from the Babylonian Talmud, which deal specifically with the Messiah. I will be citing the part of the Talmud titled Sanhedrin.

Before I get to the references I provide a summary of what this portion of the Talmud teaches in respect to the coming Messiah.

The following Old Testament are all interpreted messianically, in reference to the Messiah’s coming, functions and/or characteristics: Genesis 15:13; 49:10; Deuteronomy 11:21; Psalms 72:5, 17; 80:6; 95:7; 119:165; Isaiah 1:25-27; 18:5, 7; 23:15; 48:11; 53:4; 54:9; 59:16-21; 60:21-22; 62:5; Ezekiel 29:21; 32:14; 36:8; 37:25; Jeremiah 16:13; 30:9, 21; Lamentations 1:16; Daniel 7:13-14 25; Micah 5:1-3; Habakkuk 2:3; Zephaniah 3:11-15; Haggai 2:6; Zechariah 8:10; 9:9.

All the prophets only spoke of the coming messianic era.

The world was created because of the merits of the Messiah, i.e., for Messiah’s sake.

Some of the Messiah’s names include: Shiloh, Yinnon (“continue”), Hanina (“favor”), Menachem (“comforter”), “The Leper”.

The Messiah already exists and is sitting at the entrance of Rome stricken with leprosy, which he bandages one wound at a time in order to prepare at any moment when he summoned to appear for the redemption of the world.

The time of the coming of the Messiah and the messianic era were supposed to have already taken place right around the time Jesus appeared. This is based on the calculations which the rabbis made on the basis of texts such as Dan. 7:25 and Hag. 2:6.

The sins of the Jews caused God to delay the Messiah’s coming and the start of the messianic era.

The repentance of the Jews can actually speed up the coming of the Messiah.

Rabbis disagree in respect to how long will the messianic era last, i.e., 40 years, 365 years, 400 years, or 7,000 years.

Some rabbis believed that Hezekiah was the Messiah and that there would be no other Messiah after him. The Talmud refutes this interpretation and uses Zech. 9:9 to prove that the Messiah had not arrived yet since this prophecy was written during the time of the Second Temple’s construction. And yet Hezekiah reigned and died during the first Temple.  

If the Jews prove themselves worthy of redemption then the Messiah will appear in the clouds of heaven per Dan. 7:13-14. However, if they prove to be unworthy then he will come riding on a donkey per Zech. 9:9.

The Messiah will reign as King with the resurrected David being his comrade.
