Talmud, Christian Writings & the Name

August 19, 2025
judaism christianity god writing theology #Beautified 2025

The Talmud contains an interesting discussion concerning what should be done to the scrolls of heretics such as Christians, which contain God’s names. All emphasis will be mine.

Note the admission that the rabbis coined the term avon gilyon to ridicule the Gospels. It’s a play or pun, in fact a deliberate perversion of the Greek word euangelion., i.e., Gospel. Avon means “sin,” “iniquity,” whereas gilyon refers to a folio or margin. Hence, the written Gospel of Jesus Christ is being mockingly called a text of wickedness, evil, sin, iniquity.

What these quotations show is that Christian writings contained the names of God, which most likely included God’s sacred, covenant name YHWH.
