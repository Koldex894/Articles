JUSTIN, JEWISH CHRISTIANS & THE LAW

March 10, 2025
church-history christianity judaism torah jesus-christ salvation theology #Beautified 2025

In this post I will be excerpting the section from the holy saint Justin Martyr’s discussion with Trypho and his Jewish friends, where this blessed martyr shows that salvation comes only through our Lord Jesus Christ. Justin will also mention Jewish Christians who still kept the Law and who would still be saved nonetheless. Justin will even argue that every righteous and pious individual who lived in accord with the Law written in their hearts, but had not known Christ, will also be saved through Christ.
