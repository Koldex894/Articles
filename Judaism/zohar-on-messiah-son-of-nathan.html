Zohar on Messiah Son of Nathan

October 8, 2026
zohar messiah david judaism #Beautified

There is an interesting Jewish mystical text in the Zohar, which claims that the Messiah will be a descendant of Nathan the son of David, not Solomon’s. It even traces the Messiah’s lineage to Nathan through the maternal line, meaning through a female descendant of Nathan. What makes this all the more fascinating is that the text says that the Messiah son of Nathan was actually born during the destruction of the second temple! 

I cite here at length. All emphasis is mine.

Ohr HaChammah on Zohar 3:173b

Remarkably, Jesus is said to be the descendant of Nathan the son of David, a lineage which would have received solely from his mother who miraculously conceived and gave birth to him by the Holy Spirit while she was still a virgin:  

Besides all this, Jesus was born when the second temple was still standing.

God has truly left the Jews with no excuse for rejecting their only Lord and Messiah, Jesus the Nazarene.
