There are some important theological implications about these statements and goals of the Muslims.

If the Jews were annihilated completely it would mean that the words of Yahweh would not be true. It would mean that the Islamic Allah would be victorious over the Biblical Yahweh. It would mean that Satan had won over Yahweh. This raises serious questions about the nature of God. Who is really God? Is the Islamic Allah really another front for Satan? It is important to know that Yahweh has a history of revealing Himself. It began with Adam and Eve, continued through the time of Noah, and began to be more particular with Abraham and the beginning of the Jewish people. The history of Yahweh’s revelation involves making a covenant with Abraham and his son Isaac, and then Jacob. After that Yahweh revealed himself to Moses, Joshua, the Judges, then the prophets of the Old Testament. Yahweh spoke through the prophets about a great revelation that was to come in the person of the Messiah who would be a blessing to the world. A reading of the Old Testament tells of Yahweh’s revelations and history confirms that Yahweh did what he said he would do.

We now turn to the promises that Yahweh made to Abraham.

Since Yahweh has made certain promises we should review them in light of the Muslims hatred of the Jews. Our first reference begins in Genesis 12:

This covenant was renewed four times: Genesis 13:14-17; 15:4-6; 17:10-14; and 22:15-18.

The account in 17:10 involves the renewal of the covenant with Abraham’s son, Isaac.

The steadfast promise of Yahweh to Abraham also goes to his son, Isaac, not Ishamel. The covenant was passed on to Jacob.

The promise was renewed with Moses when Yahweh commissioned him to go to deliver the Hebrews from Egypt.

The Jews lived in the land until 586 BC when their sins were judged and they were taken into captivity into Babylon. But Yahweh did not forsake them. The prophets speak of the return of the Jews to the land. Isaiah declared, “then you will find the joy that comes from serving me. I will make you honored all over the world, and you will enjoy the land I gave to your ancestor, Jacob. I, LORD, have spoken.” (Isa.58:14)

2. Muslims who lay claim to the land are going against Yahweh. There is no divine grant of the land to Muslims. Even though the Muslims conquered Jerusalem and built a mosque there, their conquest does not mean any more than the conquest by Babylon or Assyria in ancient times. Islam will come to an end by virtue of the promise of Yahweh. “Jesus said to his disciples, "Whoever listens to you listens to me; whoever rejects you rejects me; and whoever rejects me rejects the one who sent me." (Luke 10:16) "My Father has given me all things. No one knows who the Son is except the Father, and no one knows who the Father is except the Son and those to whom the Son chooses to reveal him." (Luke 10:22)

Muslims do claim to honor Jesus, but only as a prophet. Jesus claimed to be the Son of God and if you reject Him as the Son of God, you also reject the Father. The father is Yahweh who made the covenant with the patriarchs.

3. Abraham was told that his descendants would bless the world. The world includes women as well as men. Women have not been blessed by Islam. They have been demeaned, enslaved, left in ignorance, mutilated in their youth, regarded as property, denied equality in inheritance, denied freedom to go out without a male relative, relegated to secondary positions by virtue of polygamy, raped, honor killed, and killed because of rape. The Jews have blessed the world by their intellectual achievements. Muslim science was stalled by the Ash’arite influence in which investigation of the universe was not allowed because every moment is a re-creation by Allah. The flapping wings of a bird have nothing to do with its flight. The flight is the direct action of Allah. This would mean that a jet does not fly by its engines, but by Allah. When a plane crashes Allah caused it. On the other hand, Western science has made amazing advances because we regard Yahweh as a rational being who created order and laws in the universe. Christians and Jews have been awarded many scientific honors because of their discoveries. Moreover, Jewish women have contributed to scientific advancement.

Matthew 1:23 affirms the word of Isaiah that he would be "God with us," Immanuel. At the trial before the crucifixion he was questioned by the High Priest, “…the High Priest spoke to him, "In the name of the living God I now put you under oath: tell us if you are the Messiah, the Son of God." Jesus answered him, "So you say. But I tell all of you: from this time on you will see the Son of Man sitting at the right side of the Almighty and coming on the clouds of heaven!" Jesus was very clear about who he was. This is seen in John 14 where these words appear.
