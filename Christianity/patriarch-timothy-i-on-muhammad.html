Patriarch Timothy I on Muhammad

July 4, 2025
islam christianity church-history history theology #Beautified 2025 Qur’anic Contradictions

The following excerpt is taken from The apology of Timothy the Patriarch before the Caliph Mahdi, translated by Alphonse Mingana, published by Manchester University Press, England, 1928, pp. 196-198. Timothy I (AD 780-823) was the Patriarch of the Assyrian Church of the East who made an apology for the Christian faith to the third Abbasid Caliph Mahdi.

The brilliance of the Patriarch can be seen from the fact that though he praises Muhammad before the caliph, he made sure to never call him a prophet. Instead, he simply affirmed that Muhammad walked in the path of the prophets. All emphasis will be mine
