Muhammad’s Dilemma

Herein lies the problem for Muslims.

The Quran and Islamic corpus, in total agreement with God’s Word the Holy Bible, testify that there is not a single creature that is absolutely sinless and morally perfect. The Islamic sources also agree with the Word of God that Jesus is essentially pure, being free from all sins and imperfections. What this means is that the Islamic corpus has basically identified Jesus as God in the flesh! The Muslims simply have no honest or consistent way of getting around this fact.

Here’s a breakdown of the logical implication of what both the Holy Bible and the Islamic data proclaim concerning the sinfulness of man and the moral perfection of Christ:

  1. There is no creature that is sinless.
  2. Jesus is absolutely sinless.
  3. Therefore, Jesus cannot be a mere creature but must be a divine Being who became man.
  4. Both the Holy Bible and the Quran agree that there is only one divine Being whom the Bible calls Jehovah.
  5. Jesus must, therefore, be Jehovah God in the flesh; otherwise he could not be divine, and therefore would not have been morally perfect.
  6. At the same time, Jesus is distinguished from the Father and the Holy Spirit.
  7. God’s Word identifies the Father as the only true God (cf. John 5:44; 17:3), with the Holy Spirit being described as the eternal Spirit of God (cf. Hebrews 9:14).
  8. This means that the Father, Son and Holy Spirit are all fully divine.
  9. And since Jehovah is the only divine Being, this means that the Father, Son and Spirit are all Jehovah God.
