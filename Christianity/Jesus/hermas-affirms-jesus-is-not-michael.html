In this post I will be quoting from The Shepherd of Hermas (Roberts-Donaldson translation). Hermas was a very popular writing among Christians and was even considered canonical by many. The book is intriguing in that it affirms that the Son of God is an uncreated divine Spirit who became flesh. The work further affirms that this divine Spirit created and sustains all creation by his name/authority, and is personally distinct from all of God’s glorious angels, one of whom is Michael. This means that Hermas does not view Michael as being the same Person as the Son of God, but a creature sustained by God's Son.
