Early Christians Refute Sola Fide

August 16, 2026
faith salvation christianity church-history jesus theology #Beautified

In this post I will cite some early 2nd-3d century Christians witnesses that all define saving faith as obedience to Christ’s commandments. Some of these authorities also demonstrate that the works of righteousness which Paul opposed, were those of the Mosaic, meaning that the blessed Apostle was censuring anyone who claimed that justification only comes through obedience to the Law. He wasn’t denying that saving faith which justifies is obedience to the commandments of Christ. All emphasis will be mine.
