Origen on the Father as the Source of the Son’s Goodness

May 24, 2026
theology trinity god jesus holy-spirit wisdom #Beautified

In this post I will be quoting from Origen’s De Principiis to show how this brilliant theologian interpreted the words of our Lord to the rich young ruler that God alone is good (cf. Mark 10:17-18; Matthew 19:16-17; Luke 18:18-19).

Origen explained this in respect to the Father being the unbegotten Source/Fountainhead of Deity, from which all the essential divine qualities such as goodness originate. Origen taught that the Son and the Holy Spirit eternally and fully possess the divine essence by virtue of the Son being timelessly generated by his Father, and with God’s Spirit eternally proceeding from him.

Origen stresses the point of the Person/Hypostasis of the Son being uncreated since he is God’s Wisdom and Radiance. And since Origen read in the divine Scriptures that God’s Radiance is eternal and that God has never been without Wisdom, he correctly reasoned that the Son could not be a creature that was brought into existence.

Origen also understood that the Father and the Son must be exactly like each other if they possess one and the same essence. Hence, as the Image of the invisible God, the Son must be immaterial, incorporeal, invisible, omnipotent, omniscient, the truth, the life, essential goodness, etc. Origen even appeals to Revelation 1:8 to prove that Christ is the Almighty God like the Father.
