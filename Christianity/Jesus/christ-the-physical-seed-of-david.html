In this article I will cite all the relevant NT verses which affirm that Jesus is a physical descendant of king David, making him an Israelite from the tribe of Judah. Since Jesus was born of the Holy Virgin by the Holy Spirit, this means that the Lord Jesus traces his human ancestry solely through his blessed Mother.  

Letters of St. Ignatius 

St. Ignatius was a disciple of the holy Apostles and the Bishop of the church at Antioch, Syria. He was taken to Rome where he willfully died as a martyr of the Lord Jesus Christ by being mauled by the lions. On his way there he wrote seven letters to specific Churches, which have been preserved. I quote from some of them in which the holy martyr describes Jesus as a physical descendant of David.
