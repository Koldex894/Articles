Polycarp & Justin Martyr: Every Creature Shall Worship Jesus as God

August 31, 2026
jesus god worship theology church-history christianity #Beautified

The Scriptures proclaim that God alone is to receive what the Greek calls latreuo, which is a type of worship given to true Deity:

In this post I will quote from Saints Polycarp and Justin Martyr, two holy servants and martyrs of the Lord Jesus Christ, both of whom proclaim that the risen Lord is to receive latreuo from every created being. Since God alone is to be given latreuo, this early, universal Christian practice simply confirms the fact of Jesus being worshiped as God Almighty from the very beginning of the Christian movement. It wasn’t a later development as some scholars and religious groups claim.

Keep in mind that Polycarp was also a direct disciple of the holy Apostles, including John and, therefore, his testimony carries so much weight since he would have obviously been taught the faith by Jesus’ very own eyewitnesses.

SUMMARY

Notice this consistent unbroken chain from Polycarp to Justin Martyr, two holy servants of Christ who wrote at different times and in different locations. Both of them affirm that God raised Jesus physically, bodily from the dead, and exalted him to sit enthroned in heaven alongside of him. Both of these martyrs also proclaim that Christ will physically return to judge the living and the dead based on how they responded to him, and whether they obeyed his commandments. Both holy men explicitly teach that every creature is expected to serve (latreuo) Christ, thereby giving him the very worship that the Holy Bible expressly says is to be given only to God Almighty. This is precisely what the beloved Apostles taught in their inspired writings:

Paul ascribes to Jesus the exclusive worship, which the prophet Isaiah says that all the nations of the earth must give to YHWH alone!

And:

According to Paul, this worship from every creature that Christ is to receive will take place on the day of judgment when every created being will stand before the risen Lord in order to give an account to him:

We, thus, have an unbroken written tradition that Christians the world over were worshiping and glorifying the risen Jesus as God Almighty in the flesh, testifying to his being the Son who is coequal to the Father and the Holy Spirit in essence, power, glory and majesty.
