Justin on Christ as the Incorruptible Word of God

August 24, 2026
jesus god theology church-history #Beautified

In this post I will be citing from the various works of St. Justin to show that this holy martyr taught that Christ is distinct and different from the created order. I will demonstrate that Justin believed that all created, generated things are mutable and corruptible, whereas Jesus is the very Word of God who exists before all creation and by whom creation came into being. This means that Justin placed Jesus on the side of the incorruptible, immutable Creator, not among the corruptible and mutable creation. All emphasis will be mine.
