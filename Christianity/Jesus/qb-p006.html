Qur'an Contradiction:

The Old Age of Jesus?

The ministry of Jesus lasted until he was about 33. This is even admitted by Yusuf Ali in his comment no. 388 on Sura 3:46.

But Sura 5:110 says he taught the people in (up to his) old age.

Can 33 years considered "old age" by any stretch of imagination?
