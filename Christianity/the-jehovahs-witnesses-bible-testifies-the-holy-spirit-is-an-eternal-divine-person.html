What makes the foregoing examples so problematic for the JWs is that the Holy Spirit is depicted as exhibiting specific personal characteristics alongside of other individuals whom no JW would deny are actual persons, i.e., the Spirit bears witness with the apostles, people lie to him as they do to God and the disciples, he makes decisions along with the elders and apostles in Jerusalem etc. As such, these examples cannot be explained away as personification, e.g., the Spirit is being personified as other inanimate objects are often personified in the Holy Bible, since this would mean that God and all these human individuals are being personified as well.

Therefore, the JWs cannot escape the fact that even their very own (per)version of the Holy Bible testifies that the Holy Spirit is an eternal, divine Person, and not merely Jehovah’s impersonal energy or power which he uses to bring about his will in creation.
