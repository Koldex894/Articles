In this part of our discussion we are going to contrast what the Quran says about why Allah created mankind and hell with God’s inspired Word.

Man, however, chose to rebel against God even though God made him righteous:

As a consequence of man’s rebellion and wickedness, God in his perfect justice and holiness had no choice but to punish human beings. Here Christ weeps over the fact that Jerusalem has left him with no other choice but to destroy her for refusing to recognize who he is and why he had come. Christ didn’t come for the purpose of destroying Israel, but to redeem his people from their sins in order to reconcile them back to their God:

But alas, Israel deemed itself unworthy of obtaining eternal life by refusing to embrace Jesus as their risen Lord and Savior:

In fact, the true God commands all the inhabitants of the earth to turn to him in order to be saved:

Thus, what a marked contrast between the true God revealed in the Lord Jesus Christ with the one proclaimed in the Quran. The God whom Jesus came to reveal, however, did not create hell for mankind since he loves all human beings and desires none to perish.
