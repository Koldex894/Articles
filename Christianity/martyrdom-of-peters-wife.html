Martyrdom of Peter’s Wife

June 3, 2025
church-history saints christianity #Beautified 2025

According to Clement of Alexandria, there was a tradition which stated that Peter’s wife was martyred as the blessed Apostle looked and encouraged her:  

Thus also the apostle says, “that he who marries should be as though he married not,” and deem his marriage free of inordinate affection, and inseparable from love to the Lord; to which the true husband exhorted his wife to cling on her departure out of this life to the Lord. (The Stromata, or Miscellanies, Book VII)

Eusebius cites this tradition from Clement approvingly, adding the fact that Peter also had children:

Chapter 30. The Apostles that were Married.
