The Revelation of the Gospel in Adam’s Genealogy

October 22, 2019
genesis noah gospel jesus-christ holy-spirit prophecy salvation #Beautified 2019

In this post, I am going to demonstrate how the names of the antediluvian humans, starting with Adam and terminating with Noah, all point to the Gospel of the Lord Jesus Christ. When one looks at the meaning of these names one will discover how the Holy Spirit encoded within these names the revelation of the coming of the Lord Jesus Christ, whose violent death brings the desperate rest that mankind needs from the effects of sin and the kingdom of darkness.

Here are the various lists, which include the names of the pre-flood humans:

When we look at what these names mean and try to piece them together, we then get the following message:

A man that has been appointed, a mortal who comes to purchase a people to be his own possession, being the one whom God praises, and who is himself the praised God that has descended to train up and teach mankind the paths of salvation. He has dedicated himself to fulfilling God’s will by lowering himself in status. He did this in order to experience a violent death, being thrust through by a spear, in order to bring about the rest to a world that desperately needs it.
