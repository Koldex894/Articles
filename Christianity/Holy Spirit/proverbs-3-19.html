The Doctrine of the Trinity

(Brief Study)

Submitted by: Frank DeCenso, Jr. (af664@yfn.ysu.edu)

This study is not meant to be exhaustive by any means, but rather, it is meant to provide a concise and conclusive argument for the Trinity. For a more exhaustive study, see _The Trinity_ by Edward Henry Bickersteth, and _The Biblical Doctrine Of The Trinity_ by Robert Bowman (published by The Christian Research Institute).
