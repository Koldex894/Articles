Enoch & the Deity of Christ

June 25, 2026

In this post I will be quoting from that section of the Book of Enoch called the Watchers. I will be using the following translation: The Book of the Watchers. All emphasis will be mine.

My aim in doing so is to show that what Enoch says about God is precisely what the New Testament writings say about Christ. The very functions, characteristics and appearance of God in this section of Enoch are all ascribed to the Lord Jesus, and the reason seems obvious. Jesus Christ is being presented as the Lord God Almighty who became flesh, even though he is personally distinguished from both the Father and the Holy Spirit.
