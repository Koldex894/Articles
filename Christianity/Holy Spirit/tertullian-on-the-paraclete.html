Tertullian on the Paraclete

November 18, 2026
#Beautified holy-spirit trinity

In this post, I will be quoting from Tertullian’s writings in relation to his view of the Paraclete. The references will show that Tertullian believed that the Paraclete is the Holy Spirit and that the Spirit is the third Person of the Holy Trinity, being equal in substance with the Father and the Son. Tertullian argues that this is the ancient faith passed on by the holy Apostles. Tertullian further describes the Spirit as Christ’s Vicar on earth, and believes in progressive revelation and development of doctrine. Tertullian sees these as the works of the Paraclete as he guides the Church into all truth.

All emphasis is mine.
