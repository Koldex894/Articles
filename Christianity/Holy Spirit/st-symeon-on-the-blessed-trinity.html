St. Symeon the New Theologian (Greek: Συμεὼν ὁ Νέος Θεολόγος; 949–1022) was an Eastern Orthodox monk and poet who was one of the four saints canonized by the Eastern Orthodox Church and given the title of “Theologian” (along with John the Apostle, Gregory of Nazianzus, and Saint Hesychius the Priest of Jerusalem).
