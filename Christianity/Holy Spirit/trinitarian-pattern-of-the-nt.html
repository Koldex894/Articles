TRINITARIAN PATTERN OF THE NT

April 7, 2024
trinity holy-spirit jesus-christ god theology bible christianity #Beautified 2024

The aim of this post is to quote the multiple NT references where the Father, Son and Spirit are coordinated together in contexts that presuppose the characteristics and functions they share in common, as well as to their personhood, all of which point to their essential Deity.

The plethora of such verses shows that this consistent triadic pattern is not a coincidence, but is a divinely inspired fact proving that the Trinity is not something which men made up.

Rather, the consistent grouping of only these three Persons in contexts where all of them carry out certain divine tasks and/or possess specific divine qualities shows why the early church was forced to come up with the doctrine of the Trinity.

All scriptural citations will be taken from the Legacy Standard Bible (LSB).
