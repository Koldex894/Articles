How Unitarian Logic Proves the Father Cannot be God

January 1, 2000

Illustrating how the Anti-Trinitarian logic Ends up proving that the Father can’t be God!

Sam Shamoun

Some anti-Trinitarian heretics of the unitarian flavor argue that Jesus cannot be Jehovah God in the flesh since the Holy Bible proclaims that it was the Father who gave Christ all authority in heaven and on earth, and the name that is above all names (cf. Matthew 28:18; Philippians 2:9-11). Even the life that the Son possesses within himself, and the power and glory which he receives, have all been granted to him by his Father according to the God-breathed Scriptures (cf. John 5:22-25, 26-27; Hebrews 1:6). These heretics reason that Jesus could be given all this because he is God’s duly appointed Agent who is invested with the Father’s absolute authority.

In light of these objections, I am now going to turn the tables on these anti-Trinitarians by showing them that, per their skewed understanding of what God can and cannot be and/or do, the Father can’t be God either.

According to the God-breathed Scriptures Jehovah receives an inheritance:

The Holy Bible further says that creatures actually give to Jehovah power, glory, and honor:

To make matters worse for the anti-Trinitarian, the Holy Bible says that God the Father receives power and kingship in the same contexts that Christ does!

In fact, we are even told that it is the Son who shall personally give the kingdom to the Father!

Therefore, if Jesus can’t be God because he receives things from the Father, then the Father can’t be God either because he receives things from his creation, including an inheritance.

Obviously, no one, not even these heretics, would accept such fallacious reasoning.

However, the worst is still to come for these anti-Trinitarians. Both the apostles and the righteous angels are God’s agents invested with God’s authority, and yet neither the apostles nor these righteous angels ever allowed anyone to worship them:

And yet Jesus receives the exact same worship that the Father receives from every created thing in existence!

This passage is a huge embarrassment and raises a majpr problem for anti-Trinitarian heretics since it proves that Jesus is not a part of creation, but is separate and distinct from every created being in all of creation. Christ is placed on the same side that God the Father is placed on in the Creator/creature divide. As such, Jesus must be Jehovah God Almighty (even though he is neither the Father nor the Holy Spirit) since only Jehovah is uncreated. This explains why the Father would tolerate (in fact demand) every creature in existence giving his Son the exact same worship that he himself receives.

So here is my challenge to these anti-Trinitarians. Show me a single example where any of Jehovah’s anointed earthly kings received the same exact worship from every created thing in existence like Jesus does and will continue to receive forever and ever.

Finally, the Scriptures teach that there is none like Jehovah who alone is exalted on high:

Once again, Jesus must be Jehovah God in order for the Father to exalt him to share in his unique name and status over the entire creation!

So much for this rather desperate unitarian objection.
