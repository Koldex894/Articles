Irenaeus on the Unity of the Faith

April 26, 2025
church-history theology christianity faith saints orthodoxy #Beautified 2025

I quote what the holy martyr and saint of the Church Irenaeus said in regards to the universal beliefs of Christians around the world. It is important to keep in mind that Irenaeus wrote around 180 AD, and was the Bishop of Lyons, France, being the disciples of the Bishop Polycarp, who himself was discipled and trained by the holy Apostles, specifical John.

Irenaeus affirms that all Christians throughout the world believe in the blessed Trinity, that Jesus Christ is the Lord, God, Savior and King who became flesh and was raised in his flesh into heaven.

The holy saint further attests that the universal Church taught that Christ will return to physically resurrect the dead in order judge all mankind and angels. The wicked will be assigned to everlasting fire whereas those who have done good will be rewarded with immortality. This blessed martyr even attests that the invisible God would appear visibly in a variety of forms to various individuals.

Here I am citing his work called Adversus haereses, Book I. All emphasis will be mine.
