The above passage shows that Jesus not only has the ability to resurrect people back to life, he also possesses the divine authority to transmit power through his physical body unto others in order to grant them instant healing and cleansing.

Moreover, these two cases provide further examples of Christ’s moral, ethical and ceremonially incorruptibility, since the Law prohibited coming into contact with a woman with a flow of blood or a dead body.

To do so meant that the person would become ceremonially unclean and would have to be removed from the camp of people for a period time, and only be allowed to reenter after having washed in water (cf. Leviticus 15:25-30). And yet instead of making Christ unclean the reverse occurred, i.e., the woman with a blood disorder and the dead young girl were instantly healed and restored by touching/being touched by the Lord.
