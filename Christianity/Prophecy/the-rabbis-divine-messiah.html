The Rabbis’ Divine Messiah

May 15, 2026
bible ezekiel israel judaism messiah prophecy #Beautified

The late Orthodox Jewish philosopher and scholar Rabbi J. Immanuel Schochet wrote about the Messiah where he encapsulated some of the Old Testament verses, which rabbinic tradition interpreted in respect to the Messianic Era:

A. Restoration of the Bet Hamikdash

C. End to Evil and Sin

D. Awareness and Knowledge of G‑d

E. Universal Worship of G‑d

H. Blissful Utopia: End to Disease and Death

What makes this list of references rather intriguing is that in some of them it is YHWH God himself is coming to live on earth and who performs all these miracles and functions!

By interpreting these passages in reference to the Messiah, the rabbis failed to see how this ends up confirming the Christian understanding that the coming Davidic Ruler is no mere human being. Rather, the Anointed King is YHWH God Almighty who becomes a Man in order to reign as the Son of David. At the same time, this Divine Ruler is personally distinct from another who is also identified as YHWH Almighty.

Scriptural references are taken from the Legacy Standard Bible (LSB).
