Precise Fulfillment in Jesus Christ

We are going to show here how the Lord Jesus brings about the fulfillment of all of the objectives stipulated in Daniel’s remarkable prophecy.

“To finish the transgression, To make an end of sins,”

“To make reconciliation for iniquity,”

“To bring in everlasting righteousness,”

“To seal up vision and prophecy,”

“And to anoint the Most Holy”

This either refers to the anointing of the most holy place of the Temple, whether the earthly one or its heavenly counterpart, or perhaps even both. Or it may be referring to the anointing of the Messiah. Yet despite how one chooses to interpret this phrase, the fact still remains that its precise fulfillment is found only in the Person and work of the Lord Jesus Christ:

Concluding Remarks

It is evident that, as far as the inspired Christian Scriptures are concerned, the Lord Jesus is the fulfillment of Daniel’s prediction since he came exactly when Daniel said he would, and died a violent death as stated by the prophet, one which ushered in the objectives that God had set out to accomplish such as making an end to sin and bringing in eternal righteousness.

Now for those who refuse to believe that Jesus is the Messiah they are going to have to explain how the words of Daniel were fulfilled and by whom. After all, the prophecy clearly states that the Messiah would appear at the completion of the 69 weeks decreed by God, with both the city and the sanctuary being destroyed shortly after the violent cutting off of the Messiah. Since both Jerusalem and the temple were destroyed in the year 70 AD, this means the Messiah had to have appeared and been killed before that time. There is simply no way around this.

Therefore, the only way to avoid accusing Daniel of being a false prophet is to accept the fact that Jesus is the Christ spoken of by this blessed servant of God, whose coming and death perfectly fulfilled the words of this prophecy.
