David Baron, The Servant of Jehovah: The Sufferings of the Messiah and the Glory That Should Follow (1922; repr., grand Rapids: Zondervan, 1954), 109n2 comments: “No little controversy has centered round the last line of this verse [Isa. 53:8]. But even if it be admitted that lamo… is here a plural, there would be no ground for the assertion that the subject is a collective one. The translation would be: ‘For He was cut off from the land of the living. For the transgression of my people–the stroke or punishment that should have fallen on them.
