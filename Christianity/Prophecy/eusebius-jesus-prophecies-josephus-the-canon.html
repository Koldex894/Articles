Eusebius, Jesus’ Prophecies, Josephus & the Canon

May 28, 2025
church-history canon prophecy jesus history bible christianity #Beautified 2025

In this post I will be taking excerpts from Eusebius’ Church History, Book III.

In these extracts Eusebius mentions the ministry of the Apostles, their dispersion throughout the world, and some of the books attributed to them and others which were disputed by the early Church, such as 2 Peter. Eusebius then quotes at length from the works of Jewish historian Flavius Josephus to detail all the miraculous signs and horrors that took place during the Jewish War, which culminated in the destruction of Jerusalem and the Temple.

Eusebius shows who all these atrocities were a fulfillment of our Lord’s words in Matthew 24, Luke 19:41-44 and 21. This indicates that the early Christians took these prophecies as referring to Christ coming in great power and glory to destroy Jerusalem for their sin of rejecting and crucifying him.

Eusebius further mentions what Josephus wrote in regards to the canon of the Hebrew Bible. All emphasis will be mine.
