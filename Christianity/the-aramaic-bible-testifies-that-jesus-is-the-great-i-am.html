In this post I will be quoting from the Aramaic Bible in Plain English, which is the translation of the Aramaic Bible known as the Peshitta.

It is apparent that the translator took this phrase to be a divine appellation, understanding it be a divine self-ascription affirming God’s timeless and uncreated existence.
