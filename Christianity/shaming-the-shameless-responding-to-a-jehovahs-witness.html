MAINA

RESPONSE

Seeing that Maina erroneously assumes that Jehovah is a singular Person, not just a singular Being, and further believes that the Father ALONE is Jehovah, as he himself says at the very conclusion of his “rebuttal,”

The God-breathed Scriptures clearly identify the Father, Son and Holy Spirit as Jehovah God Almighty, while also affirming that they are not one and the same Person. For a helpful study on the biblical witness to the Triunity of God we highly recommend the following resource by Robert M.
