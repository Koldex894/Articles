How Can I Support the Ministry of Answering Islam?

January 1, 2000

Make a Donation to Support the Ministry of Answering Islam

If the information provided by Answering Islam has helped you to find answers to your questions, or you have been blessed in other ways through the work done by this ministry, please consider supporting us through prayer or donations. Even small amounts will help us to continue with our work.

At this time we can receive financial support in two forms:

  • USA only: Send a cheque to: 

    Advancing Native Missions
Post Office Box 5303
Charlottesville, VA 22905
USA

    Place "Donation for Answering Islam" in the purpose line, and make the cheque payable to "Advancing Native Missions".

    Alternatively, you may use their online giving page.
    [To the right of the phrase "Donate to:" you will find a drop-down box. Choose "Specific Projects".
    A new line "Project" will appear. Select"ANSWERING ISLAM" from the drop-down list.
    Fill in the boxes that will appear, and then click the button "add to basket".
    Finally, go to the "CHECKOUT" link near the top right of the window.]

    Your donation will be tax-deductible and you will receive a receipt for it.

    If you want to support our work from another country (UK, Germany, etc.) please inquire and we might be able to give you a non-profit organization in your country through which you can support Answering Islam.


  • Internationally: Electronic donation via PayPal:

    On the PayPal form the recipient will appear as "Globalink".
