  5. Yet both the Father and the Holy Spirit are identified as God as well (Cf. Genesis 1:1-2; 2 Samuel 23:2-3; Job 33:4; Psalm 104:30; 139:7-12; John 17:3; 1 Corinthians 8:6; 2 Corinthians 3:17-18; Ephesians 4:6; Hebrews 9:14).
