SOLA FIDE: SALVATION BY GRACE THROUGH FAITH ALONE PT. 1

December 17, 2019
salvation faith theology gospel jesus-christ romans eternal-life #Beautified 2019

In this series of posts, I am going to quote all the verses, which stress that salvation is a free gift of God which he bestows because of his grace to all who will put their trust in the Lord Jesus.

In some of the passages, people were healed of various ailments because of their faith in Christ. Hence, even though these examples are not directly about everlasting life, they still emphasize the centrality, necessity, and sole sufficiency of believing in the Lord Jesus to obtain all spiritual graces.

Gospels and Acts

I begin with the testimony of the four Evangelists, namely, Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John. And since Luke also wrote Acts, I will be quoting from that inspired witness as well.

In the next segment (/2020/07/18/sola-fide-salvation-by-grace-through-faith-alone-pt-2/), I will be posting verses from the New Testament (NT) epistles to see what these inspired writers taught concerning this subject.
