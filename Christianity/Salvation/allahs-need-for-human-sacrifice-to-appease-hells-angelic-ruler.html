Ransoming Muslims From the Ruler of Hell

Muslims are faced with a major dilemma since a ransom assumes a payment given to release someone. Therefore, the question that they need to answer is, to whom exactly does Allah make the ransom in order to redeem Muslims from being tortured in hell?

The answer may come as a shock.

According to the Quran, hell has a ruler called Malik which, ironically, is the Arabic word for master, ruler and/or king!
