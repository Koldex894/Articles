Flour Instead of Blood for Atonement?

November 19, 2026
#Beautified atonement sacrifice

Anti-Christian polemicists/apologists appeal to Leviticus 5:11-13 to prove that blood isn’t necessary for atonement since flour suffices to atone for sin.

The problem with this claim is that a completely different picture emerges when the passage is read in its immediate context:

The context makes it clear that the reason for offering fine flour for a sin offering is because the person is too poor to afford a lamb, a goat, or even two turtledoves or pigeons. The context further shows that the flour atones because the priest adds it to the sacrifices offered on the altar.

In other words, it is still the blood of the animals and birds that makes atonement for sin and guilt. This shows that the flour, by itself, could not atone unless and until it was placed on the altar with the sacrifices.
