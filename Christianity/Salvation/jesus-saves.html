The neophyte references Psalm 146:3 to show that neither princes nor a son of man can help or deliver which therefore disqualifies Jesus from being the Savior since he is both a Prince and the Son of Man. We have already addressed the distortion of this particular text, so there will be no need to repeat ourselves.

The dawagandist further cites Psalm 60:11, 108:12, and 118:8 to show that man cannot save and uses this to again prove that Jesus cannot be the Savior since he is a man.

The assumption underlying this entire article is that Jesus is nothing more than a human being and as such is disqualified from being the Savior according to the OT. However, this underlying assumption conveniently ignores the NT teaching that Jesus is more than a man since he is the unique Divine Son of God who became a human being.

In fact, everything that Psalm 146 says concerning Yahweh the NT says of Christ!
