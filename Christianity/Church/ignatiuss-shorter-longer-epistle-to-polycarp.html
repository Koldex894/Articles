It contains the shorter (Syriac) and longer recensions of St. Ignatius’ letter to St. Polycarp, the Bishop of Smyrna. The post quotes the shorter recension first. All emphasis will be mine.

Shorter and Longer Versions

COMMENTS

Both the shorter and longer recensions of Igantius’ epistle to Polycarp agree with the middle since all three affirm:

Father and Son are personally distinct from one another.

Jesus Christ is true God who became Man, transcending time and therefore timeless, being the invisible, incorporeal Son of God became flesh and inhabited a physical body.

Jesus Christ’ death, physical bodily resurrection, and his physical ascension/return to the Father.

The Bishop is appointed to be the head of the Church as Christ’s representative. Christians are, therefore, required to fully submit to him, as well as to the elders/presbyters and deacons who are subject to the Bishop.
