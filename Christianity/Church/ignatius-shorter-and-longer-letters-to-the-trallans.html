The following translation is taken from: Trallians – Roberts-Donaldson English Translation (Shorter and Longer Recensions).

It contains the shorter (Syriac) and longer recensions of St. Ignatius’ letter to the Trallans. The post quotes the shorter recension first. All emphasis will be mine.

Shorter and Longer Versions

Chapter I.-Acknowledgment of Their Excellence.

Chapter II.-Be Subject to the Bishop, Etc.

Chapter III.-Honour the Deacons, Etc.

Chapter IV.-I Have Need of Humility.

Chapter V.-I Will Not Teach You Profound Doctrines.

Chapter VI.-Abstain from the Poison of Heretics.

Chapter VII.-The Same Continued.

Chapter VIII.-Be on Your Guard Against the Snares of the Devil.

Chapter IX.-Reference to the History of Christ.

Chapter X.-The Reality of Christ’s Passion.

Chapter XI.-Avoid the Deadly Errors of the Docetae.

Chapter XII.-Continue in Unity and Love.

Chapter XIII.-Conclusion.

COMMENTS

In complete agreement with the middle recension, the shorter and longer versions of Ignatius to Trallans confirm the following beliefs:

Father and Son (along with the Holy Spirit) are personally distinct from one another.

Jesus Christ is true God who became Man, being the eternal Son of God that later became flesh from the holy, blessed Virgin Mary.

Jesus Christ’ death, physical bodily resurrection, and his physical ascension/return to the Father.

All true believers must be in complete submission to the Bishop, presbyter and deacons, since there is no is the head of the Church without them.
