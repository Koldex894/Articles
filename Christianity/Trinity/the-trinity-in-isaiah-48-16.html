The Trinity in Isaiah 48:16?

May 19, 2026
isaiah trinity christianity god israel #Beautified

There are some Christians that believe that there is a specific verse in the book of Isaiah, which explicitly points to the Trinity. It is Isaiah 48:16, the context of which I quote so the readers can see why this passage is taken as evidence for the Trinity.

Isaiah 48

The Speaker all throughout has been YHWH, which suggests that it is YHWH who says that he has been sent by the Lord YHWH along with YHWH’s Spirit.

On the other hand, there are others that claim that the last line of verse 16 is actually the prophet speaking, and not YHWH. They base this off of what immediately follows, where Isaiah quotes the words of YHWH again:

They argue from the fact that there is a break between vv. 16-17, where the prophet reintroduces the words of YHWH, that it is no longer YHWH speaking at the conclusion of v. 16.

In response to this objection, I will quote from Isaiah himself where the inspired prophet will often break the flow of YHWH’s proclamations without an indication that it is YHWH who is still speaking.
