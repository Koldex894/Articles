Eusebius: Jesus as Eternal Wisdom & Angel

April 24, 2025
church-history jesus-christ trinity wisdom angels theology #Beautified 2025

Eusebius of Caesarea was a noted historian who wrote a comprehensive history of Christianity, beginning with the eternal generation of the Word and his subsequent human incarnation from the blessed Virgin, and culminating with events taking place at his time (circa 324 AD).

Eusebius states that Christ is the eternally generated Wisdom/Word of God who existed before the ages and before every creating thing came into existence. Hence, this renowned Christian historian rejected the notion of Christ being produced out of nothing, or from things created. Like those who came before and after him, Eusebius believed that Jesus is the uncreated Word of God who was begotten out of God’s very own uncreated essence and Being.

Eusebius further affirms that Jesus is that very God who appeared throughout the Old Testament, often manifesting to them as a Man, being that same One called the Angel of the Lord. Eusebius also sternly repudiates the assertion that these divine encounters were appearances of created angelic beings.
