Another OT Appearance of the Prehuman Christ

As promised /2018/07/29/the-trinity-in-genesis-35-and-22/, in this post I am going to show that the Lord God who appeared to Adam and Eve in the garden was actually the preincarnate Christ.

According to the NT writings, the Lord Jesus is the eternal Word of God who makes God known to mankind.
