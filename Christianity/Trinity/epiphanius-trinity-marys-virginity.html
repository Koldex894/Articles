Epiphanius, Trinity & Mary’s Virginity

May 25, 2025
mary trinity church-history orthodoxy catholicism theology #Beautified 2025

The extracts in this post are taken from the English rendering of The Panarion of Epiphanius of Salamis Books II and III. De Fide (Second, revised Edition), translated by Frank Williams [Brill, Leiden-Boston 2013], Volume 79, from the section titled “Against Antidicomarians 1 58, but 78 of the series”, pp. 617-636.

In defending Mary’s perpetual virginity, Epiphanius a host of related issues such as Joseph’s children from a previous marriage, the possibility of Mary being the Woman of Rev. 12, the martyrdom of James the Just, as well as the timeless begetting of the Son and the Deity of the Holy Spirit. All emphasis will be mine.
