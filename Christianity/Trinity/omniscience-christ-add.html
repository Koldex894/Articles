This means that Christ must be omniscient and omnipotent, and therefore fully God in essence, since this is the only way that he could be able to possess all the treasures of God’s wisdom and knowledge.

This brings me to my last point. The Lord Jesus claims to be just as incomprehensible and omniscient as the Father is:

Note that Jesus says that he knows the Father in the same way that the Father knows him, which is why he is the only One qualified to make the Father known to others. Since the Father happens to be unsearchable and incomprehensible by nature, this means Christ is claiming to be omniscient. And by also stating that the Father is the only One who truly knows the Son, Christ is basically claiming to be unsearchable and incomprehensible. This explains why Jesus says that only the Father knows him since it requires someone with an omniscient mind to fully comprehend and understand the Son.

The foregoing list confirms the historic Christian position that Jesus is truly God in essence who became (and continues to be) a human being. In light of this fact, it shouldn’t come as a surprise that the inspired revelation would portray Christ as perfectly knowing all things while also learning and growing in wisdom since he is both divine and human at the same time.
