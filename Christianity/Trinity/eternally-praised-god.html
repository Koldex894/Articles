In this post we are going to present a chart listing all the verses where the inspired NT authors offer doxologies1 to the risen Christ, and place them alongside the ones which are directly addressed to God.

We will also quote those where a doxology is offered to God through Jesus Christ his Son.

This will show that the very first believers worshiped Jesus in the exact same way that God is worshiped.

In fact, apart from Christ one will never find a case where a doxology1 is given to someone other than Yahweh God Almighty.

This in itself proves that the first Christians (many of whom were monotheistic Jews) were worshiping Jesus as God, obviously because they undoubtedly believed that he was/is God in the flesh.

In other words, Jesus must be God in the flesh in order for the Father to command everyone to give him the same exact praise and glory which only Yahweh is supposed to receive.
