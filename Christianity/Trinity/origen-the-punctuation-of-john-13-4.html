Origen & the Punctuation of John 1:3-4

August 22, 2025
bible bible-study gospel jesus-christ trinity church-history theology #Beautified 2025

In this post I will be sharing quotes from Origen in respect to his exegesis of John 1:1-4, particularly in relation to the punctuation of vv. 3-4 where it states that what came be in the Word was the life, which is the light of salvation. As the readers will see, Origen did not interpret these verses to imply that the divine Person of the Word was created at a specific point in time. Rather, Origen is clear that the Wisdom/Word who subsequently became the Man Jesus is uncreated by nature, and that there was never a time when he did not exist.

The citations will prove that this brilliant theologian affirmed that the Son is eternally begotten, not made, and that he along with the Spirit is beyond all time and all age, since both of them along with the Father are eternal by nature. All emphasis will be mine
