More Biblical Proofs that Jesus Christ is Jehovah God Almighty

Since the Hebrew Scriptures describe Jehovah as the Lord of the whole earth,

It isn’t surprising that he would also be identified as the Judge of all the earth:

In fact, the prophetic witness expressly teaches that Jehovah himself will come to the earth and judge all the nations:

This shall be the day that Jehovah will recompense everyone for all they have done:

And since Jehovah alone knows all the hearts and thoughts of mankind, he will be able to render perfect justice to everyone:

This is where it gets rather remarkable.

The New Testament not only proclaims that Jesus is the Lord of all creation,

But also expressly teaches that the Father judges no one, since all judgment has been given to the Son:

This is why we are told that Christ will gather all the nations before his judgment seat, on the day he comes to sit upon his glorious throne, so that everyone may give an account to the Lord Jesus:

Even more astonishing is the fact that the following texts apply the language of Isaiah 40:10 and Jeremiah 17:10 to the Lord Jesus!

In this last passage, not only is the risen Lord said to do what Isaiah and Jeremiah claim Jehovah will personally do when he comes, but also the very names that belong only to God Almighty,

Are applied to Christ!

Nor is this the only place in Revelation where these unique divine titles and characteristics are ascribed to the Lord Jesus:

Here we have Christ claiming to be the Alpha and Omega, the first and the last, the beginning and end, as well as the Almighty!

With such overwhelming evidence, how can any honest reader of God’s Word deny that the inspired Scriptures emphatically proclaim that Jesus Christ is Jehovah God Almighty in the flesh (even though he is not the Father or the Holy Spirit)?
