Jesus Christ – The Human Incarnation of Jehovah God Almighty

December 18, 2018
jesus-christ trinity god christianity bible theology holy-spirit #Beautified 2018

In the following chart, I will cite passages where the very unique names, titles, characteristics and/or activities, which are attributed to Jehovah God Almighty in general, or to the Father specifically, are ascribed to the Lord Jesus Christ. This in turn provides further biblical confirmation that Christ is Jesus God in the flesh, even though he is not the Father or the Holy Spirit.
