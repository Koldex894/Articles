Nyssa on the Angel of God

July 4, 2025
angels jesus-christ trinity moses church-history orthodoxy theology #Beautified 2025

I quote what the great St. Gregory of Nyssa wrote in respect to the divine Angel that appeared to Moses being the prehuman Son of God in his work titled Against Eunomius, Book XI.

Gregory refutes the assertion that God spoke through a created angel whim he used as his medium, and denies that the Son being the Angel implies he is a creature. This great saint also affirms that texts such as Romans 9:5 all describe Jesus Christ as God Almighty in the absolute sense. All emphasis will be mine.
