August 29, 2026

I share what St. Gregory the Wonderworker wrote in respect to the monarchy of the Father and the glorious, blessed and most holy Trinity: On the Trinity. However, it must be noted that scholars think that this work is dubious, spurious, inauthentic, etc., since they do not believe it is actually from Gregory. Certain scholars actually think that this actually comes from Apollinaris of Laodicea, and therefore is to be dated around 380 AD. With that said, I now quote the fragment. All emphasis is mine.

(NOTE: The electronic text obtained from The Electronic Bible Society was not completely corrected. EWTN has corrected all mistakes found.)
