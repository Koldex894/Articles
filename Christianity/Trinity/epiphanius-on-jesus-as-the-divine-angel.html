April 30, 2025

The extracts in this post are taken from the English rendering of The Panarion of Epiphanius of Salamis Books II and III. De Fide (Second, revised Edition), translated by Frank Williams [Brill, Leiden-Boston 2013], Volume 79, pp. 362-66.

Epiphanius affirms that Jesus is the divine Angel who appeared in the pillar of cloud and fire during the Exodus to accompany Moses and the Israelites. All emphasis will be mine.
