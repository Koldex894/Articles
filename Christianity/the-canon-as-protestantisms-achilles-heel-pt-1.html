The Canon as Protestantism’s Achilles’ Heel Pt. 1

August 2, 2026
canon church-history christianity bible theology #Beautified

In this post I will be quoting sections of Eusebius’ Church History in respect to the various debates and disagreements over the canonicity of particular books, which were/are considered sacred. Some of these writings did not make it into the canon but were highly regarded and viewed as inspired by some if not many early Christians.

The purpose in highlighting these debates is to show the dilemma that Protestants who affirm Sola Scriptura face since one cannot have the Scriptures as the sole infallible rule of the faith without first knowing what those Scriptures are. This is especially the case during the first centuries of the Church when the Christians had not even decided on what the limits of the biblical canon was.
