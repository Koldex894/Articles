The following is taken from: Romans – Roberts-Donaldson English Translation (Shorter and Longer Recensions).

It contains the shorter (Syriac) and longer recensions of St. Ignatius’ letter to the Romans. The post quotes the shorter recension first. All emphasis will be mine.

Shorter and Longer Versions

The readers can see that both recensions of Ignatius’s epistle to Rome affirm the following:

The personal distinction between the Father and Son, and that both are to be praised and worshiped.

Jesus Christ is truly God and Man, the Son who became flesh from the seed of David.

Jesus Christ died, rose again and ascended into heaven.

The Eucharist is the body and blood our God Jesus Christ.
