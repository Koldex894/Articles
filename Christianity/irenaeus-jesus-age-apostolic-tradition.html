Irenaeus, Jesus’ Age & Apostolic Tradition

September 11, 2025
church-history christianity jesus jesus-christ bible gospel #Beautified 2025

I cite a section from St. Irenaeus’ Adversus haereses (“Against Heresies”), where he explicitly states that the elders/bishops personally received and were taught a tradition from the Apostles such as John that our Lord was older than thirty, and was in fact in his forties when his earthly ministry terminated. St. Irenaeus even quotes John 8:57 to prove that this tradition is anchored in both Sacred Scripture and Tradition. All emphasis will be mine.
