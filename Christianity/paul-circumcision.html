Refuting One Taqiyyist’s Assault Against the Blessed Apostle Paul

January 1, 2000
islam christianity bible quran hadith theology #Beautified

Sam Shamoun

Noted taqiyyist and dawagandist Shabir Ally engaged in a debate with former Muslim turned Christian Thabiti Anyabwile. The debate, titled “How Can We Find Forgiveness From A Holy God?,” was part of the Dubai Muslim Christian Dialogue and took place on Sunday, May 19, 2013.

During the debate Ally engaged in his usual assault against Paul and the consistency of the NT writings by trying to pit the Apostle against Luke, claiming that a contradiction exists between what Paul wrote in his epistles with what Luke claims this blessed and holy servant of the risen Lord Jesus said and did.

Ally contended that Paul’s companion Titus refused to get circumcised since the Apostle was dead set against the necessity of circumcision and observing the Law of Moses for salvation (cf. Galatians 2:1-3). Luke, on the hand, has Paul circumcising Timothy and depicts the Apostle as observing the Law of Moses, even going as far as to pay the money for the completion of a Nazirite vow which four Jewish believers had undertaken, a vow which would have included offering animal sacrifices such as burnt and sin offerings upon its completion (cf. Acts 16:2-3; 21:18-26; Numbers 6:14). Shabir Ally took this as evidence that Luke sought to present Paul in a more favorable light, despite the fact that his depiction contradicts what the Apostle himself wrote concerning circumcision and obedience to the Mosaic Law.

We have decided to address these assertions in order to explain what these passages mean in their immediate and over all contexts. Moreover, this will also provide further evidence that Ally has no interest in understanding the message of the Holy Bible, or in trying to handle the text of inspired Scripture in a fair and honest manner. His only concern, it seems, is to present the teachings of the Holy Bible in the most negative fashion possible.

First, Paul wasn’t against circumcision per se, but was against the insistence of some of the Jews that the Gentiles had to get circumcised as a sign of their commitment to keeping the Law of Moses for their justification and salvation. Paul, like Luke, proclaimed a person is saved by the grace of the Lord Jesus which one receives as a free gift through faith and repentance towards Christ:

Again, as the context of Galatians shows, the reason why Titus refused to get circumcised is because certain Jews were making it an issue of salvation, i.e. a Gentile had to get circumcised and keep the same laws of Moses in order to be saved and justified. To Paul, and the rest of the Apostles and followers of Christ such as Peter, John and James, this was a perversion of the Gospel of grace, a false gospel which brought condemnation instead of salvation:

This is why Paul could publicly rebuke Peter for associating with these false Jewish believers and pulling away from the Gentiles, so as to appease these Jews by not offending them, even though Peter knew they were wrong since he believed that a person is justified by repenting and believing in the Lord Jesus Christ, not by works of the Law:

This now brings me to my final point. According to the book of Acts, the way in which ethnic Jews showed their love to Christ for saving them was by continuing to observe the Law of Moses, yet not for their salvation or justification, but as the necessary sign and evidence of their being saved by the grace of Christ. The Gentiles, however, were set freed from keeping some of the commands of Moses such as circumcision, since they were not ethnically Jewish and were therefore not expected to observe those signs and instructions which singled out the nation of Israel from all other races.

This can be seen from what James, the brother of the risen Lord, went on to say in the same council which Peter spoke up in defense of Paul and Barnabas:

This is why he agreed to James’ suggestion since Paul wanted to expose the lie which the Jewish believers heard concerning the blessed Apostle supposedly going around to the Jews of the Diaspora and instructing them to set aside the Law of Moses:

With that said, we now issue the following challenges to Ally. We want him to produce a single statement from Paul’s writings where this blessed and holy servant of Christ instructed ethnic Jews who had turned to Christ to set aside commands such as circumcision, Sabbath observance, dietary restrictions etc. We further challenge him to quote Paul telling Gentiles who believed in the Gospel that they shouldn’t get circumcised, observe Sabbath etc., once they understood that these practices do not save or justify them. In other words, we want Ally to provide evidence that Paul would have had a problem with Gentiles who understood that they were not saved by the works of the Law, but by the grace of the Lord Jesus which they received as a gift through faith in him, going ahead and observing specific OT instructions such as circumcision, dietary restrictions etc. If Ally can’t produce such statements then he needs to publicly apologize to Christians for trying to smear the name of one of the greatest Christian evangelists who ever lived, an individual whom his own false prophet Muhammad doesn’t even come close to in terms of holiness, righteousness, purity, devotion, service etc.

So much for Ally’s shameful butchering of God’s inspired Scriptures and slander against the blessed servant and holy Apostle of our risen Lord and Savior Jesus Christ.
