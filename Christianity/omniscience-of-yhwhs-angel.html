The problem here is that Stafford has grossly misread the text since the Angel isn’t asking due to his ignorance Rather, the Angel is rather interceding with God in order to move him to show compassion, which is precisely what God, and all because of the Angel’s intercession!

Therefore, the Angel wasn’t asking out of ignorance but was rather interceding on behalf of the Jews in order to cause YHWH to remove his displeasure and wrath from his covenant people.
