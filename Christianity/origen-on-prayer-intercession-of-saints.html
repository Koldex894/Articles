Origen on Prayer & Intercession of Saints

June 12, 2025
prayer saints church-history theology christianity #Beautified 2025

Here will be quoting from the English translation of Origen on Prayer, published by Christian Classics Ethereal Library, Grand Rapids, MI, pp. 25-29.

Origen lists four different types or moods of prayer, which can be broken down as:

  1. Request.
  2. Prayer.
  3. Intercession.
  4. Thanksgiving

Origen permits that requests, intercessions and thanksgivings can be made to both God and the saints. He also allows for intercessions and thanksgivings to be offered to other than saints.  

Origen insists, however, that prayers are to be made to God alone, showing that he wasn’t against the invocation of saints, whether they be angels or spirits. Rather, what he condemned and objected to is offering worship to any other being besides the one true God, which is what he meant by prayer.  

Even more remarkable is the fact that Origen prohibited, in fact sternly censured, any believer who would pray directly to the Son! Instead, he argued that Christians ought to only pray to the Father through the mediation and intercession of Christ.

Hence, Origen not only condemned praying to the saints but also expressly forbade doing so to Christ!
