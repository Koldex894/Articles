Honoring the Son

We saw earlier in the first part of the rebuttal how the prophet Daniel prophesied that the nations would worship one like a Son of man, with Christ claiming to be that Son of man according to the inspired Christian Scriptures.

Therefore, by making himself out to be that same Son of man spoken of by Daniel, Christ was basically stating that he is the divine, eternal King whom all the nations were required to worship in the same way that God the Father is to be worshiped, a point which he explicitly makes elsewhere:

Note that Christ doesn’t say that the Father demands everyone to honor the Son as they honor a prophet, or one’s parents etc., but that they are to give him the same honor that is given to the Father himself!

This explains why we find repeated examples of Christ receiving worship from individuals all throughout his earthly life, such as the wise men that came to visit the young Christ child,

The blind man whom Christ miraculously healed,

The leper whom Christ cleansed,

As well as from his disciples after seeing him walk on water and sovereignly control the winds and waves,

Amazingly, these are functions which the OT ascribes to Yahweh alone!

This further explains Christ’s insistence that his followers must love him unconditionally, more than anyone and anything, including their own lives:

This kind of love can only be rendered to God, and yet this is what Jesus demanded for himself!

We thus have Christ demanding and receiving the same worship God receives in recognition of his being the unique Son of God and divine Son of man, contrary to Abualrub’s assertion.
