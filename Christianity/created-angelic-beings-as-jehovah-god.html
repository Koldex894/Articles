DISMANTLING ANOTHER ANTI-TRINITARIAN SMOKESCREEN

In this post I intend to refute a typical claim by anti-Trinitarians that all throughout the Hebrew Bible there are angelic creatures, which are identified as Jehovah due to their being God’s authorized emissaries.

The assertion is made that a created angel whom Jehovah sends to speak on his behalf can refer to himself as Jehovah God. This is not because the spirit creature is Jehovah, but because he has been authorized to speak on behalf of Jehovah.  

I will prove that this is not what the inspired Scriptures teach since it is not a group of angelic creatures that speak as Jehovah God, but rather it is one unique Messenger that does so. And the reason why he does is because that particular Angel is not a spirit creature but the eternal Word and Son of God in his prehuman existence.

Unless indicated otherwise, I will be employing the New World Translation of the Holy Scriptures (Study Edition).

Notice how the Angel is clearly identified as the Jehovah God who sees what takes place and who is able to create life and grant individuals’ numerous offspring.
