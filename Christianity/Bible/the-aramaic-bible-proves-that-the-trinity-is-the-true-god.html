In this post I will be quoting from two English translations of the Syriac translation of the Holy Bible, which is called the Peshitta. I cite this particular ancient version for the express purpose of showing that the Aramaic term for God, namely Alah/Alaha, is used for both the Father and the Son. I will also use it to prove that Jesus is identified as God Almighty who became flesh, the eternally begotten Son who is one in essence with the Father and the Holy Spirit.

At this point I will be quoting The Peshitta Holy Bible Translated, translated by Glenn David Bauscher, published by Lulu Publishing, 3rd edition 2019. Here is the online link: https://biblehub.com/hpbt/genesis/1.htm. What makes this translation rather intriguing is that it renders Jesus’ ego eimi or “I AM” statements in the most literal fashion by translating this phrase as “I AM THE LIVING GOD.” It further translates the Aramaic word MarYah as “the Lord Jehovah,” taking the expression as a compound term consisting of Mar (Aramaic “Lord”) and Yah, which is the abbreviation of God’s sacred name known as the Tetragrammaton (“four letters”), namely, YHWH (yod heh waw heh).
