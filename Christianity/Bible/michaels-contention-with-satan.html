Michael’s Contention With Satan

April 13, 2020
angels satan moses apocrypha bible canon midrash #Beautified 2020

In the epistle of Jude, the inspired writer brings up a dispute that took place between the archangel Michael and Satan over the body of Moses:

This is a difficult passage to interpret since Jude includes information not found in the canonical books of the Holy Bible, particularly the issue of Michael’s rebuking Satan over Moses’ body, a story not find in the inspired Scriptures.

The following commentaries help explain the point Jude is seeking to make and why he chose to bring up this dispute between the archangel and the devil:
