In this post I will be comparing the rendering of the New American Standard Bible 1995 edition (NASB1995) with the New King James Version (NKJV) in regards to some of the common texts employed to affirm the Deity of the Lord Jesus Christ.

I do so since, in my estimation, these two versions represent the best translations of the two different textual streams of the Greek [N]ew [T]estament.

The NASB1995 is based primarily on what is typically referred to as the eclectic Greek Text, which prioritizes the Alexandrian textual stream and the earliest Greek papyri of the NT books. The NKJV, on the other hand, is based on the Byzantine or Majority text, which is based on the majority of the extant Greek copies of the NT.
