Submitting to the Bishop

February 24, 2026
bible god israel moses torah theology #Beautified

The Holy Bible is clear that the inspired Scriptures were never meant to be the private possession of the common folk, but were to be entrusted to qualified individuals appointed by God. These were the ones whom God authorized to teach and interpret the oracles of God to his people, in order to avoid anyone from misinterpreting and twisting the revealed Word.

Old Testament Authority

According to the Hebrew Bible, God entrusted the priests with the Mosaic Law and any subsequent revelations in order to instruct the people in them:

This explains why God severely rebuked and threatened the priests whenever they strayed or perverted themselves. After all, a corrupt priesthood would result in a corruption of the meaning of God’s Word, which in turn would result in God’s people being misled and deceived:

New Testament Authority

Even Christ recognized the teaching authority of the Pharisees and scribes, despite the fact that their traditions contradicted the Word of God:

“Then some Pharisees and scribes came to Jesus from Jerusalem and said, ‘Why do Your disciples break the tradition of the elders? For they do not wash their hands when they eat bread.’ And He answered and said to them, ‘Why do you yourselves transgress the commandment of God for the sake of your tradition? For God said, “Honor your father and mother,” and, “He who speaks evil of father or mother is to be put to death.” But you say, ‘Whoever says to his father or mother, “Whatever you might benefit from me is given to God,’ he need not honor his father.’ And by this you invalidated the word of God for the sake of your tradition. You hypocrites, rightly did Isaiah prophesy of you: “This people honors Me with their lips, But their heart is far away from Me. But in vain do they worship Me, Teaching as doctrines the commands of men.”’” Matthew 15:1-9 

The Lord then promised to replace these wicked rulers with his own scribes and leaders in order to guide the true people of God aright:

Christ promised to send out the Apostles and their followers in the power of the Holy Spirit in order to correctly recall what he had taught them, as well as to make known all the additional revelation which the Spirit would reveal through them: 

The Apostles then appointed bishops/elders to replace them and to preserve the deposit of faith, which was entrusted to them by the same Holy Spirit which also filled them as he did the holy Apostles and prophets: 

The blessed Apostles further expected and commanded that believers fully submit to these Spirit-filled leaders of the Church:

After all, our Lord himself said that when a person accepts and submits to anyone whom he sends, then s/he is submitting to both him and to the Father. He further warns that rejecting the one whom he has authorized over his church is the same as rejecting both the Father and him: 

Therefore, the risen Lord set up his Church with her Spirit-filled leaders to preserve and interpret the Holy Scriptures. The Lord Jesus never intended for the Holy Bible to be placed in the hands of the masses for them to interpret the sacred oracles according to their own understanding:
