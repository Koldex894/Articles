In this post I will be listing all of the verses where Jesus is described as the Son of God. The purpose isn’t to exegete the meaning of the texts, but to simply provide a handy list of all the pertinent references to Christ being identified as God’s unique Son. I will also include statements where Jesus and the inspired writers refer to God as the Father in relation to Christ. I will begin with Mark’s Gospel and end it with John’s Revelation (also known as the Apocalypse).

All scriptural references are from the Revised Standard Version (RSV).

The term pais and its derivatives can refer to a servant and/or a child, as the following Greek lexicon affirms: Strong’s Greek 3816.

Therefore, Luke may have chosen this word since it functions as a double entendre, an expression which highlights the fact that Christ is both a Son and a Servant. I.e., Jesus is God’s unique Son who chose to become the Father’s Servant for the express purpose of accomplishing the salvation of the world.
