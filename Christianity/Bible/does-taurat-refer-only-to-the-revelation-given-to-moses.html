Refuting Another Muslim Canard

An oft-repeated Islamic objection is that the Taurat which the Quran repeatedly mentions is not identical to the Old Testament Scriptures, but refers to the original revelation given to Moses.

There are at least two main problems with this argument.

First, the Quran nowhere claims that the Taurat was given to Moses. This is an assumption that is being read back into the Islamic scripture, and is based on the Jewish-Christian definition of the term and/or later Muslim tradition.

This brings me to my second problem. There have been Muslim scholars of both the past and present which have candidly acknowledged that the word Taurat (Torah) can and does in fact refer to all of the Judeo-Christian Scriptures, and not just to the books ascribed to Moses.
