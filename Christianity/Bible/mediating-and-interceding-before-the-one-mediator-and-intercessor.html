MEDIATING AND INTERCEDING BEFORE THE ONE MEDIATOR AND INTERCESSOR

November 18, 2022
prayer salvation atonement jesus-christ saints bible theology #Beautified 2022

The God-breathed Scriptures exhort believers to pray and intercede for the salvation of all mankind since they have one mediator who has offered himself as a ransom to procure the redemption of the entire human race:

Jesus’ mediation ensures that God will accept and hear the prayers of his people to save others since it is God’s express desire that none perish but for all to be saved through faith in Jesus Christ.

In this post I will quote examples where Jesus either healed, saved or blessed individuals due to the intercession/mediation of others.

These cases will illustrate how specific persons sought and beseeched the risen Lord to grant mercy, healing and/or forgiveness to those particular individuals which they interceded for, and which Christ in his mercy granted.

This will show that it is God’s express will that believers mediate before the One Mediator, and intercede with him for the sake of others so that the risen Lord may bestow his mercies and spiritual blessings upon those for whom intercession is made.
