Proving that Jesus is not the Archangel Michael

July 11, 2019
jesus-christ angels trinity theology bible christianity #Beautified 2019

In this post, I am going to show a quick way of refuting the belief of Jehovah’s Witnesses, and other Arians, that Jesus is the archangel Michael. I will be using the book of Hebrews to make my case against this false, heretical view.

According to the inspired author, Christ is the exact representation of God’s infinite Being:

Now compare that with the following Psalms:

Since Jesus is said to be the EXACT representation of God’s infinite, uncreated Being, and since NONE of the heavenly beings resemble Jehovah, and not a single one of them can do the works that Jehovah does in the way Jehovah does them, nor are they capable of sustaining all things by the word of their power, this shows that Jesus cannot possibly be a mere spirit creature named Michael.

After all, Jehovah’s Being is uncreated and infinite. Moreover, characteristics such omnipotence, omnipresence, and omniscience are essential aspects of God’s very own Being. As such, no finite temporal creature can be the EXACT imprint/copy of Jehovah’s Being.

This is precisely why the Psalms plainly state that there is no god like Jah. And yet Jesus IS EXACTLY LIKE JEHOVAH, proving that he is no mere creature.

That’s not all. In Hebrews 1:6, the inspired writer cites Deuteronomy 32:43. Note what that specific verse says:

Pay careful attention to the fact that God commands ALL (not some, not most) of his angels to worship his Firstborn.

Now this places the Arians in quite a dilemma.

Since the original context of this Old Testament citation is about the gods/angels of God worshiping Jehovah, which is then applied to Jesus in Hebrews, this means that Jesus CANNOT be a mere angelic creature, and therefore CANNOT be the archangel Michael.

Here is a break down of the logic behind my argument in order to bring out with the implication of these texts more clearly:

  1. ALL the gods/angels of God are commanded to worship Jehovah.
  2. Michael is one of these gods/sons of God, and is therefore included in this command to worship Jehovah.
  3. Hebrews 1:6 applies this to the Son, meaning that it is the Son whom ALL the angels must worship.
  4. Since the original context clearly affirms that Michael is among those commanded to worship Jehovah, this proves that Jesus IS NOT the archangel Michael, but must be the very God whom Michael and the rest of the host are commanded to worship.

Biblically, exegetically, there is simply no way of getting around the force of these texts.

So much for the Arian claim that Christ is the archangel Michael.
