The Talmud on OT Authorship

July 2, 2025

The following excerpts are taken the from the Babylonian Talmudic tractate Bava Bathra, and deal with the authorship of the Old Testament books. One interesting position mentioned by the Baraita is that there were rabbis who actually believed that God had Moses record his own death and burial in advance in order to complete the writing of the Torah. Others believed that it was Joshua that wrote that particular section.

Moses is further credited with writing the book of Job. Even more interesting is the fact that certain rabbis had to refute one specific rabbi who denied that Job existed and thought his book was nothing more than a parable. All emphasis will be mine.
