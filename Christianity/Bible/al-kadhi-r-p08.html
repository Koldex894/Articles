A response to Chapter 8 Jesus in the Qur'an

There's not much to say in response to what Mr. Al-Kadhi wrote.

Christians neither look to the Qur'an, the Hindu Vedas, or any other book to discover the truth about Jesus. Anything beyond the scriptures is speculation.

The Jesus described in the Qur'an is not the same Jesus as described in the Scriptures. That's why the Christians rejected Muhammad as a prophet of God.

In the Scriptures, Jesus said, "I am the way, the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me" (John 14:6). "For the Son of Man came not to be served but to serve, and to give his life a ransom for many" (Mark 10:45).
