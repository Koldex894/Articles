Christ: The Divine Morning Star

September 23, 2026
angels bible david god jesus revelation #Beautified

In John’s revelation, the risen and glorified Christ appears to him and announces that he is the bright morning star:

Accordingly, some have taken this to mean that Christ is a created angelic being since the book of Job speaks of the heavenly sons of God as morning stars:

In this post I will prove that Jesus is not a spirit creature like the angelic beings, but is the Creator and Sustainer of all creation. This will show that Jesus is the Creator of the heavenly spirit beings who brought them into existence and is personally sustaining them by his word.

To begin with, both Job and the rest of the Hebrew Bible explicitly affirm that YHWH alone created and preserves all things:

Remarkably, the epistle of Hebrews has God the Father ascribing the above Psalm to his Son!

Hebrews makes it clear that Jesus is the uncreated Son of God who is personally distinct from the Father, and yet identical to him in essence. The Son created and sustains all creation, which includes all the periods of time and the angels, who reigns forever as the God-Man and whom all the created angelic being must worship.

In fact, the Hebrew Bible expressly states that none of the heavenly beings resemble YHWH, are as mighty as YHWH, and can do the works that YHWH does in the exact same manner that YHWH does them:

And yet Hebrews affirms that the Son is the exact imprint/copy of God’s infinite, uncreated substance who personally oversees and preserves all creation by his powerful command.

Our Lord himself taught that he only does whatever the Father does in the exact same way that the Father does them:  

This means that the Son perfectly resembles his Father, is just as mighty as his Father, and does everything the Father does in the exact same way that his Father does them!

Hebrews is not the only inspired writer that identifies the Son as the immutable Creator and Sustainer of the entire creation:

Therefore, Jesus is not a created morning star like the angelic host. Rather, the Son is THE original, archetypal, uncreated Morning Star whom the rest of the stars are but mere shadows and mirrors of.
