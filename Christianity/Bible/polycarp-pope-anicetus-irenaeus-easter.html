Polycarp, Pope Anicetus, Irenaeus & Easter

June 2, 2025
easter church-history passover catholicism saints jesus-christ #Beautified 2025

Church historian Eusebius recorded the early controversy that arose among the faithful in respect to the celebration of Easter.

Some Christians such as Polycarp insisted that the Church should follow the example within the Gospels by observing it on the fourteenth day of Nisan, which is the date fixed by Scripture for commencing the feast of the Passover. Since our Lord was betrayed and crucified around that time, it only makes sense that these holy men of the faith would want to continue that tradition.

Others insisted that the Church needed to follow the custom of the Apostles in regards to celebrating Christ’s resurrection on the actual day he rose, which was on a Sunday, and which they began calling the “Lord’s Day” (Kyriake) because of it.
