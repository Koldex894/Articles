Here is what we have discovered thus far:

  1. Jehovah God is the Alpha and Omega, the first and the last, the beginning and the end.
  2. Jehovah God is coming quickly.
  3. Jehovah God searches the innermost thoughts and hearts of every individual in order repay each person according to his/her ways.
  4. Jehovah God sent his angel to testify to his servants concerning end time events.
  5. And yet at the same time, Jesus is the One who claims to be the Alpha and Omega, the first and the last, the beginning and the end.
  6. Jesus is the One that is coming quickly.
  7. Jesus is the One who searches the innermost thoughts and hearts of every individual in order repay each person according to his/her ways.
  8. Jesus is the One that sent his angel to John to announce what shall shortly take place.

Now since there obviously cannot be two Alphas and Omegas, firsts and lasts, or beginnings and ends, Jesus must therefore be Jehovah God Almighty in the flesh (even though he is not the Father or the Holy Spirit). There is simply no way of getting around this clear, explicit biblical witness.

In light of this, how will the JWs reconcile what even their own translation says concerning Jesus being the Alpha and Omega, the first and the last, the beginning and the end, when they deny that he is Jehovah God Incarnate? Will they go the route of disbelief and claim that the Holy Bible contradicts itself? Or will they be willing to humble themselves by acknowledging that they have been wrong all this time and finally accept the fact that the inspired Christian Scriptures do present Jesus Christ as Jehovah God Almighty who became a man for our redemption?
