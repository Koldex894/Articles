COMMENTS

In perfect agreement with the middle recension of Ignatius’ epistle to Ephesus, both the shorter and longer versions affirm the following:

Father, Son and Spirit are personally distinct from one another.

Jesus Christ is true God who became Man, the uncreated Word and Son of God that existed before time, who then later became flesh from the holy, blessed Virgin Mary.

The Bishop is the head of the Church and stands in place of Christ, which is why all true Christians must be in perfect union with him.

The Eucharist is the sacrifice of Christ, which believers must receive in order to obtain immortality and the cure for death.
