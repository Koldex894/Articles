  1. Gr. ton … archegon tes zoes. The word archegos is used four times of Christ in the NT. Here and in Heb. 2:10 it denotes him AS THE SOURCE OF LIFE OR SALVATION (since “life” and “salvation” are both represented by one Aramaic word , hayye, the phrase used here is practically synonymous with ton archegon tes soterias in Heb. 2:10; cf.
