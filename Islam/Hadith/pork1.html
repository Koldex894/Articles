Shabir generalizes and states that the "Bible" prohibits the eating of pork. It is quite correct to use the term "Bible" in reference to a specific book or sections of the revelation. Yet, in the case of eating pork, it is only A SPECIFIC SECTION of the Holy Bible that forbids it. A careful reading of the entire Bible leads us to conclude that this prohibition was intended for a specific group of people. It wasn't intended FOR ALL PEOPLE AT ALL TIMES. More on this below.
