Warding off Black Magic / Jinn / Evil Spirits

January 1, 2000
islam allah quran hadith sunnah angels prayer theology #Beautified

We received the question:

  • Would you please let me know ... how to ward off the evil jinn or spirit or black magic?

Dear Friend,

Thank you for your inquiry concerning "how to ward off the evil jinn or spirit or black magic." I live in an African country where many people live in fear of evil spirits. People here have devised many ways to attempt to overcome the evil powers of darkness. Many wear charms or amulets containing Qur'anic verses to protect them from evil spirits. Many place fetishes in their homes, drink potions and make frequent visits to the marabouts ("holy" men). Some believe that if they say, "bi ism Allah" (in the Name of God) before entering a house or a car, they will be protected from accidents. Others pour out offerings to "personal protector spirits" in an attempt to ward off evil. But all these ideas come from men who ignore the Holy Scriptures of the Prophets---that is, the Word of God. What matters is this: What does the Word of the one true God have to say about protection from evil spirits?

God's Word teaches us that Satan and his demons are real and able to perform certain kinds of miracles. Evil spirits are powerful, but they are not all-powerful! The Holy Scriptures teach that we do not need to live in fear of these evil spirits if we listen to and obey God, who is infinitely more powerful than all evil jinn and black magic put together. The Lord God wants us to put our confidence in Him, not in religious rituals, or fetishes or men. Anything that replaces simple faith in the true and living God is an idol. Faith in special prayer formulas or charms or spiritual guides cannot give us victory over the devil---as a matter of fact, the devil often uses these very things to accomplish his evil purposes.

In the holy Injil we read, "Be strong in the Lord and in his mighty power. Put on the full armor of God so that you can take your stand against the devil's schemes." (Ephesians 6:10,11). An important piece of the "armor of God" is "the shield of faith, with which you can extinguish all the flaming arrows of the evil one." (Eph. 6:16) The Scripture teaches us that it is only through faith in the living God and His Word that we can overcome the spiritual forces of evil. The most important question concerning faith is not: how much faith do you have, but who do you have faith in?? God's Word tells us that there is only One who can give us victory over evil spirits. Do you know who that One is? Yes, it is Jesus the holy son of Mary. He came from heaven, was born as a perfectly holy man; there was no sin in Him. The Holy Scriptures tells us that the reason Jesus appeared was "to destroy the devil's work." (Injil: 1 John 3:8) Jesus came to deliver us from our sins, from the power of the devil, and from eternal judgment. Jesus was more than just one of the prophets. He was the powerful, eternal Word of God who "became flesh and made his dwelling among us." (Injil: John 1:1-3,14). The Scripture says that the Lord "shared in [our] humanity so that by his death he might destroy him who holds the power of death---that is, the devil---and free those who all their lives were held in slavery by their fear of death." (Injil: Heb. 2:14,15)

Yes, Jesus had authority and power over evil spirits and over every kind of power---Satan, sickness, nature, death---because He was the "Ruh Allah" (the Soul of God) and the "Kalimat Allah" (the Word of God) who came from heaven. That is why Jesus only had to speak the word and the evil spirits fled from the demon-possessed man. Today, Jesus is in heaven where He possesses "all authority in heaven and on earth" (Injil: Matthew 28:18) Whoever believes in Him will never again need fear evil spirits. All of God's unlimited power resides in the Lord Jesus. Therefore, if you believe in Him and receive Him as your Savior and your Lord, you will no longer need to fear anything: not death, not life, not evil spirits, not witches, not the present, nor the future! You will no longer need to wear charms or consult spiritual guides because the Lord Jesus will protect you. The Scripture says: "In [Jesus] Christ all the fullness of the Deity lives in bodily form, and you have been given fullness in Christ, who is the head over every power and authority!" (Injil: Col. 2:9,10) Jesus Christ has conquered Satan, and in His name every form of evil can be overcome. There is power in the Name of Jesus. But before you can experience that power and deliverance you must believe in Him and receive Him as your Savior and Lord.

God wants us to trust in Jesus "who is the head over every power and authority" instead of trying to appease the lesser powers and authorities of this world. God promises to give His Holy Spirit to all those who believe in Jesus as the all-sufficient Savior who came from heaven to die on a cross for our sins and to rise triumphantly over death three days later. And whoever has the Holy Spirit in his or her heart can claim this wonderful promise found in the Injil: "The One who is in you (the Holy Spirit of Jesus) is greater than the one who is in the world (Satan)." 1 John 4:4

Let me close this letter by saying that I have several friends here in Africa who used to live in constant fear of evil spirits and black magic. But they no longer live in fear. They have been set free by the truth of God's Word. If you would like to hear their personal stories about how the Lord Jesus has delivered them and given them peace, let me know and I'll ask them to write out their testimonies for you.

If you have any further questions, feel free to write to me directly.

Brahim Sene
