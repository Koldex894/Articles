Jesus is declaring Islam to be a false religion!

By

Dallas M. Roark, Ph.D


A wonderful experience is happening to many Muslims around the world. Jesus is appearing in visions and dreams to all kinds of people—men, women, imams, and even jihadists. In these dreams He is telling people to “Follow Me.” Their encounter brings a sense of peace, joy, and awareness that their sins are forgiven. At the same time their lives are now facing persecution, and death for their commitment to Jesus and leaving Islam.

The visions and dreams of Jesus are calling Muslims to reject Islam in spite of persecution and the possibility of death.

Jesus’ words to people when He was here in the flesh indicate Islam’s view of Him is false. The Muslim world has been closed to the love of God and the good news about Jesus as the Son of God has been suppressed for centuries. God is breaking the barriers put up by imams through the centuries and is coming directly to many people and appearing to them in dreams and visions. They have been responding to these dreams by the thousands in Muslim countries and even in Saudi Arabia where no followers of Jesus are allowed.

Jesus is more than a prophet which Islam believes Him to be. Consider the words of Jesus when he was here in Israel:

When Jesus said “no one goes to the Father except by me” Jesus is absolutely rejecting Islam, Buddhism, Hinduism, Mormonism, and whatever other religion one may think. God came in human form to reveal his love, to bring forgiveness of sins, to heal broken lives, and promise life everlasting in his presence. No other religion does this. He is the Way—there is no other. He is the Truth—do not believe a falsehood. He is the Life—do not choose death.

This may sound like a prejudicial claim, but if Jesus is God in the flesh as he claimed to be, then he alone knows how to have a relationship with God. Buddha was not God, Mohammed was not God, and the many gods of Hinduism are not the Creator. Moreover, God came to love us, to help us, to bring joy to our lives, to have forgiveness of sins, and to let us know that He is the way to everlasting life.

One of the real issues in life is to know the Creator. We cannot see an eternal infinite Being, and we cannot comprehend infinity. This impossibility is overcome in God taking on human form and living among us in a way that we can understand. Jesus relates to this in the comments he made about His Father.

The truth about Jesus is given to us in the Gospels where it is available to many people, but it has been suppressed in Muslim countries. Jesus goes beyond the barriers and meets people directly and invites them to follow Him. And they are doing it. They are searching for other believers to find more information about Jesus either by word of mouth or by the gift of a Bible which they can read and learn more about Jesus.

In Islam women are second class citizens. In the Son of God there is equality. Women are having visions and dreams of Jesus as well as men. Women are a creation of God and share equally in his love. Jesus healed Peter’s mother-in-law, healed a widow’s son to life, raised Jairus’ daughter from death, cured a woman’s blood issue, and healed a woman from demon possession. As a result of God’s love men are commanded to love their wives as they love themselves. What a wonderful gift that God has given in marriage for a man and a woman to love each other.

Jesus was God in the human form, born of the Virgin Mary without a human father, and she conceived by the power of God’s command to her.

This event of the Messiah, the Savior, fulfills the many prophecies (fore-telling) of the Old Testament concerning the Incarnation of God.

In Islam one never knows whether Allah has forgiven one’s sins. One must wait until Judgement to know whether it will be paradise or hell. Jesus came to deal with our sins and Islam does nothing about our sins. He is the Way, the Truth, and the Life and is the way to heaven in the presence of the eternal God.

Just before he was crucified Jesus was questioned by chief priests, elders, and teachers of the Law. They were jealous of Jesus, his message, and his miracles, and they wanted to kill him.

It is strange that Islam rejects secular history telling of the crucifixion of Jesus as well as the three different times that Jesus told his disciples that he would be put to death. Muslims are taught that someone else was crucified instead of Jesus.

After the crucifixion, death, and resurrection, Jesus appeared to his disciples to confirm his statements and he gave them a command to go into all the world and preach the Good News of God’s love and salvation for all mankind.

If you want forgiveness, if you want everlasting life, if you want to know God who loves you, you have to ask Jesus to come into your life. Simply lift your heart and voice and say: Lord Jesus, come into my life, forgive my sins, and be my Savior.
