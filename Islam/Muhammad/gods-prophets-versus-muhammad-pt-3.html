God the Father and His Children

 God’s inspired emissaries describe God as a spiritual Father who spiritually begets or adopts spiritual children. They viewed spiritual fatherhood or filiation as an essential aspect of God’s Being.

I will list here all the various groups that are said to be God’s children.

Muhammad

Islam’s prophet rejected any notion of his god being a spiritual father to anyone. Muhammad taught that the highest relationship that a person could have with his deity is that of a slave to a master:

The Jews and Christians say: We are sons of Allah and His loved ones. Say: Why then doth He chastise you for your sins? Nay, ye are but mortals of His creating. He forgiveth whom He will, and chastiseth whom He will. Allah's is the Sovereignty of the heavens and the earth and all that is between them, and unto Him is the journeying. S. 5:18 Pickthall

And they say: The Beneficent hath taken unto Himself a son. Assuredly ye utter a disastrous thing Whereby almost the heavens are torn, and the earth is split asunder and the mountains fall in ruins, That ye ascribe unto the Beneficent a son, When it is not meet for (the Majesty of) the Beneficent that He should choose a son. There is none in the heavens and the earth but cometh unto the Beneficent as a slave. S. 19:88-93 Pickthall
