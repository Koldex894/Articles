"The Submitters" are also called: "Khalifites" or the "Number 19 group".

The Submitters give a different perspective on many topics. They stress that we should follow God only and accept his word in the Qur'an only and to reject the Hadith as man-made inventions. They also hold that the Qur'an is authenticated by God with a numerical miracle. And though I personally think they have a lot of good points - in some way their approach to Islam is similar to one of Evangelical Christianity and their approach to the Bible as the sole foundation of the Christian faith -, most Muslims hold them to be heretical because of their rejection of Hadith and their belief that two of the Qur'an verses are false.

A collection of responses the their claims and other similar ones.
