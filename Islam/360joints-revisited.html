The 360 Joints Issue Revisited

Still staggering around in the dark

Jochen Katz

After having been humiliated so badly on this issue, Osama Abdallah would have done better not to revisit this topic, but he just could not leave it alone.

Originally, Abdallah tried to convince people that Muhammad's saying about the human body containing 360 joints is proof of the divine origin of Islam, since this was unknown at the time, and only discovered and proven to be true in 1995. His original claim, propagated on his site between 3 June 2001 and 27 May 2004 is documented and discussed in this article.

For about half a year after the publication of our article, he invested much effort in counting joints. During this time he published many insults against us and claimed that he will destroy us and humiliate us by proving us wrong. However, in the end, Abdallah gave up and removed that article, and instead claimed that those narrations are weak/doubtful hadith, see this article. As time went on, he changed those pages several times, becoming ever more a rejecter of the vast majority of hadiths.

For his rejection of the hadiths he was then attacked by orthodox Muslims, and a substantial fight developed, in which the 360 joints hadiths played some role. Having been ship-wrecked so badly in his attempt of proving the 360 joints hadiths to be true scientifically, it becomes his main challenge to the orthodox Muslims, see the below given quotations. In those articles and announcements Abdallah calls the 360 joints hadith weak, doubtful, and even false, i.e. fabricated. He declares that he only believes in hadiths that are mutawatir. And he states that this is not a mutawatir hadith (mutawatir is the highest degree of authenticity and trustworthiness in the evaluation of hadiths).

However, it is lonely out there when one attacks the hadiths that are an essential foundation of Islam. Apparently, the constant opposition to his approach, even by respected Muslim leaders, was too hard on him to sustain. Therefore, he recently made a complete turn-around and has now an amazing new opinion on these hadiths. In that newest version of his article, he has the statement: "This Hadith (Saying of Prophet Muhammd) is a Mutawatir (genuine....among the very well known ones) one."

I have no problem with changing an opinion if presented with good evidence. I have done so myself on various points. The problem is that Abdallah only changes his opionion when the outside pressure on him becomes too great, not when he is presented with good new arguments. We showed him that this hadith is false, and he attacked us and insulted us for months for daring to question it. At the end, he changed his opinion and then claimed for about two years that this hadith is false. Later, he was fighting with the orthodox Muslims for months and accusing and insulting them (and they him), and now he joins them again in claiming that this hadith is mutawatir, i.e. that it has the highest degree of authenticity after all, but he disagrees with them on its meaning.

In the Islamic "science of hadith", the soundness or authenticity of a narration is determined mostly by examining its chain. It has little to do with its content. It is quite obvious that Abdallah declared this hadith to be false and fabricated for the one and only reason that he was unable to harmonize its content with modern science. He claimed that it is NOT mutawatir because he could not face the fact that its content is wrong. Now, after he thinks that he found a way to interpret it "spiritually" instead of scientifically, he suddenly claims again that it is mutawatir. However, nothing has changed in its transmission in the meantime. His acceptance or rejection of Islamic narrations are mere expediency, and have nothing to do with objective scholarship.

Since Abdallah has written about this issue in so many places, he forgot to remove or rewrite all his earlier pages on the topic when he published his new insights, resulting in the amusing fact that he is contradicting himself by currently propagating that this hadith is not mutawatir and even false (here) and that it is mutawatir (here).

Be that as it may. What is his new interpretation about, and does it have any merit? We are going to quote it here in full, since there is a high probability for it to disappear again soon, just like the earlier versions.
