Muslim Desparation Leads to E-Mail Fraud


Most people will know, that is very easy to forge the sender address of an email, making the message appear to originate from any valid or invalid address. Nearly all commercial spam messages have an invalid sender address.

On May 20, 2003 we received well over 3,200 bounce messages at the feedback email address of our website.

We are used to email attacks that have the sole purpose to annoy us, to clog up our system and waste our time. We have sophisticated custom software to delete thousands of similar messages within minutes, or to block specific domains from being able to send anything to us. We can always trace the i/p address of the machine that originated the spam and we always contact the network provider responsible. Network providers always respond quickly to complaints of ‘electronic terrorism’ and take appropriate action. In all these years we have never bothered to talk about such attacks, but this case exhibits some interesting ‘psychology’. This one was not sent to us, but sent in our name to many others.
