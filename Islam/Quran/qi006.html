Qur'an Contradiction:

The Event of Worship to the Golden Calf


In the last contradiction we were already looking at idol worship and the golden calf was mentioned in Sura 4:153. Now let us have a closer look at this specific story.

In Sura 20:85, Allah told Moses

Allah did not place any blame on Aaron. Aaron admitted that he did no wrong:

(20:90).

Since Moses knew this (because Allah told him already), why did he place the blame on Aaron?

(20:92). And why did he drag him by the hair (7:150)? These two accounts contradict. According to 7:151, Aaron was partly responsible for the sins of his people because Moses prayed for Aaron's forgiveness. And this time (contrary to his other confession), Aaron admits to idol making/idol worshipping in verse 150

But seemingly he gave in and did as they demanded.

Furthermore, in 7:149, the people repented about worshipping the golden calf before Moses returned, but according to 20:91 they refused to repent but rather continued to worship the calf until Moses came back.

This is all rather confusing.
