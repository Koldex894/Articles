Al-Qurtubi on Q. 66:12 & Mary as Muhammad’s Wife

November 21, 2026
#Beautified mary quran

I cite the interpretation of Q. 66:12 by the renowned Sunni exegete, al-Qurtubi. His exegesis deals with Mary confirming the words and books of Allah, and cites a tradition from Muhammad that claims that Christ’s Mother will be one of his wives in paradise. Al-Qurtubi also brings up the variant readings/recitations of this verse, showing that the Quran has not been preserved from textual variations and corruption.
