2. The Theological Test

Unlike many of my Muslim brothers and sisters, I do not scrutinize the teachings of all of the Prophets of God based on the criteria put forth by someone who claims to be a Prophet. I look at those who claim to be Prophets and scrutinize them, and their message, using the criteria established by God's Prophets over the centuries.

In other words, I study the "revelations" of all "would be" Prophets based on the writings and teachings of the Biblical Prophets. If the "would be" Prophet's teachings contradict the teachings of the Prophets of God [as Muhammad's teachings do], I reject him.
