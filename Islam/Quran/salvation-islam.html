Salvation According to Islam:

By good works, faith alone, vicarious punishment, and/or Muhammads intercession?

A follow up reply to MENJ

Sam Shamoun

Now that we have addressed MENJs criticism of what the Holy Bible teaches regarding salvation (see Part 1), whether it comes by good works or through the death of the Lord Jesus, we want to focus our attention on Islam and apply MENJs arguments to his own religion.

In many places in the Quran, believers are promised that if they do what is right and fear their Lord then they will be granted entrance into paradise. If they dont then hell will be their abode:

This clearly presupposes that salvation according to Islam is the result of having true faith and doing good works.

Moreover, certain reports claim that a Muslim who dies confessing the Shahadah, the Islamic profession of faith that there is no god except Allah, will enter paradise even if s/he has lived a grossly immoral life!

According to the above, Allah does not and will not accept the repentance of a person at death.

There are other narrations claiming that Allah will cast Jews and Christians into hell in the place of the Muslims!

Allah is going to ransom Muslims from their sins by punishing the Jews and Christians in their stead, which is nothing more than a form of vicarious atonement! Moreover, the narrations from Riyad as-Salihin help explain why Muslims are saved by faith in the Islamic creed alone in spite of being grossly immoral and wicked the Jews and Christians will be punished for the sins of these Muslims!

The hadiths further teach that Muslims are saved not on the basis of their good works, or because a Jew or Christian will be punished in their place, but because of Muhammads intercession:

Muhammad even stated that his intercession would be for those Muslims who commit major sins, i.e. adultery, murder etc.

What this essentially means is that Allah will forgive rapists, adulterers, murders etc. all because of Muhammads intercession, showing that it really doesnt matter how a Muslim lives, whether s/he decides to live a righteous life or a very wicked and immoral one. All that matters is that s/he dies professing faith in Islamic monotheism since this is all that is needed for Allah to grant such a person entrance into paradise.

Yet Muhammads intercession introduces another contradiction with the Quran since it expressly denies that there is any intercessor besides Allah:

It even contradicts other texts and narrations where Muhammad himself didnt know whether he was saved, let alone whether he could save anyone else!

Conclusion

After professing faith in Islamic Tauhid, the punishment of Jews and Christians in place of Muslims, and the (alleged) intercession of Muhammad, if there is still need of doing good works and good deeds for forgiveness of sins, then the above factors are without purpose, for there has already been the way of pardoning our sins, i.e. through good works as mentioned in the Quran. Or, if there is no need to do good works and good deeds after all the above issues, then it means that Muslims are free to do crimes and sins (which is precisely what the so-called sound ahadith say).

Therefore this is, quite expressly, a doctrinal paradox and if the Muslims continue to insist that good works save mankind, they will have to come to the conclusion that both the Quran and the so-called authentic sources of Islam contradict one another.
