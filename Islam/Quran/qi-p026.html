Qur'an Difficulty:

When Commanded Pharaoh the Killing of the Sons?

Sura 40:23-25 says,

From this, we learn that Pharaoh ordered the killing of the sons of the Jews when Moses approached him with the truth.

But Sura 20:38-39 says,

This means that Pharaoh commanded his men to kill the sons of the Jews when Moses was a child. Moses had not yet received any revelation from God.
