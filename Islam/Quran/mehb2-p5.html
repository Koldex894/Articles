Comments: The above mentioned "refined theory", puts to rest the earlier theory that the universe will continue to expand infinitely forever. Incidentally, if we accept the big bang, do we really need God? Does the Qur'an agree with Compton's this time?

Please read and visualize, how beautifully the revealed verses explain this "refined" scientific theory of the repeated expansions and contractions, in everyday terms that could be easily understood by the laity, with a vocabulary of the sixth century.

Comments: The Arabs of the revelation era, were quite familiar with the rolls of the Holy Scrolls which their Jewish neighbours had in their homes. A scribe would select a scroll of a particular "Book", very meticulously and slowly unroll it and when done with, he would roll it back. He would repeat the same process day in and day out. What better analogy there could be for the repeated expansions and the contractions of the Universe in an era of ignorance and naivete!

This passage has nothing to do with the oscillation of the universe. If we read this ayah in context, we see that it is talking about the end of the universe and final judgement. Most Muslim scholars including Syed Maududi reach this interpretation.

Comments: Today, the contemporary science tells us that the Universe (all that is between the Heavens {plural} and the Earth) has it's existence for "an appointed term", and that term is supposed to be 80 billion years. Allah alone knows the veracity of that figure. The scientists who have declared this particular number must have announced it based upon a rational scientific computation.
