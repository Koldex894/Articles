The Quran is telling us that Allah set mountains on the earth to prevent earthquakes and this is completely wrong! The mountains that Allah "set" on the earth are of the same rock strata as the earth below them! Additionally, if mountains are supposed to prevent earthquakes, I suggest that they are doing a very poor job. Some of the earths greatest seismic activity can be found in areas with mountains including the west coast subduction zone in North and South America, the Zagros Mountains in the Middle East, and the Himalaya Mountains in South Asia.
