“Boulevard of Islamism: An Example of How Islam Is Spreading and Impacting Europe”

An Interview with Mireille Valette, a Swiss Writer and Human Rights Activist

Translated by Jacob Thomas

Introduction

On 25 January, 2012, the Algerian essayist and author, Hamid Zanaz interviewed Mireille Valette, the author of “Boulevard de l’islamisme: L’essor du radicalisme musulmans en Europe, illustré par l’exemple.” An Arabic version of the interview appeared in “Al-Awan,” an online reformist Arabic journal, on Sunday, 20 January, 2013 (*). Here is my translation of the Arabic edition of the Interview.

Hamid Zanaz

Does Islam constitute a serious menace to the West?

Mireille Valette

H. Z. You do emphasize the diminution of the freedom of expression.

H. Z. In your book, you provide examples for this pressure toward self-censorship

H. Z. Is Switzerland on its way to Islamization?

H. Z. By writing “Don’t Panik” (Don’t Be Afraid!) the rapper Medine, and Pascal Boniface, the sociologist, sought to portray the fears and the prejudices that are encountered by young Muslims, and generally, by immigrants living in the suburbs of the major cities. On the other hand, you wrote in “Boulevard de l’islamisme” that “a growing number of immigrants demand the continued practice of a petrified Islam in Europe, the very ‘Islam’ that has been responsible for the sclerosis that afflicts their homelands.”

H. Z. Is the West capable to deal with this systematic aggression that you have described, and how?

H. Z. How do you explain the Western passivity vis-à-vis this conquering Islam?

H. Z. May we point to a Western tendency for a voluntary blindness and bondage?
