All truth is God's Truth

Here is an important question.

Muslims affirm that the Qur'an / Islam encourages to seek knowledge.

What happens if that knowledge doesn't match what the Qur'an teaches?

I strongly believe that "all truth is God's truth." That also means that God will not contradict himself in the "natural" revelation of history and science and in the "special" revelation of his written word.

But if the Qur'an contradicts what we so clearly know from history or science, does this indicate that maybe the author of truth in the natural realm and the author of the Qur'an might not be the same?
