Regarding Responses to Articles in the Series "Second Thoughts On Common Islamic Assumptions"

The author of this series is an extremely busy person and therefore unable to write individual answers to the many responses we receive to his articles.

Some of responses will be replied to publically on this web site, linked as "further discussion" at the bottom of the respective article. We promise, however, that every response we receive will be read carefully by the author. We will do our best that at least those messages that are not only commenting on the material but asking questions and requesting an answer will also receive a personal response from some team member of Answering Islam.

For our feedback email address(es), see this page.
