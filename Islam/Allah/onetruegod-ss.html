The One True God

January 1, 2000

Sam Shamoun

Shabir Ally has written a small article trying to prove that the only true God of the Holy Bible is the very One who sent the Quran as his final scripture for mankind. By trying to connect the god of Islam with the God of the Holy Bible, and quoting Bible references to do so, presupposes that the Holy Bible is authoritative. Shabir's approach means that the Holy Bible is the criterion that one must appeal to in order to verify the Quran. Yet by the same token, if the Holy Bible is authoritative enough to verify the Quran then it is just as authoritative to falsify it. Here, we will demonstrate how the God-breathed Scriptures which Ally appeals to falsifies the Quran and proves that Muhammad was not a true prophet.

RESPONSE:

The God who spoke through the prophets of the Hebrew Scriptures, the true God to whom Jesus prayed, is the One who relates to believers as a loving Father:

He is also the Father of Jesus, the Son of his love, whom he sent to save mankind:

Note Jesus' prayer here in John 17. Christ looked to heaven and prayed to his Father, saying that he is his Son who gives eternal life to all that God gives him, that he was there with the Father in heavenly glory even before the world was created, and that the Father has loved him from before the world! It is quite obvious that Jesus doesn't pray like Muslims, nor does his prayers comport with Islamic theology.

Moreover to deny that Jesus is God's beloved Son is to accuse God of bearing false testimony, that God lied to the people when he testified that eternal life comes from believing that Christ is his Son:

In fact, anyone who denies that God is Father and Jesus is the Son is a liar and the antichrist:

We will see next that this is not the same God proclaimed in the Quran.

RESPONSE:

There are many problems with trying to pass off the god of Islam as the true God of the Holy Bible, one of which being the fact that Muhammad's Allah is not a father to anyone. Allah is a lord who demands all creatures to come to him as slaves, no more and no less:

Furthermore, the Quran denies that Jesus is God's Son and threatens to destroy anyone who would say otherwise:

What this proves is that the Quran is not the last scripture sent by the true God of the Holy Bible. Rather, the Quran is a false book written by one who sought to usurp and corrupt the true message of the true God, thereby preventing people from finding the salvation which comes from the One who alone is truly God, namely Yahweh Elohim:
