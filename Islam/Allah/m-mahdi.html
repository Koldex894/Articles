MAHDI

Believed to appear in the end-times, and that Jesus, who is also to appear in the end-times, will be a follower of the Mahdi, and prays behind him. Some, however, believe that Jesus and the Mahdi are the same person. All proof texts are based on hadiths. The Mahdi is believed to be a member of his household (Ahlul-Bayt), from his daughter Fatima. Shi'ites claimed that he is the son of Imam Hasan al-Askari, while the Sunnis believed that it is someone else. The Shi'ites believed that the Mahdi is alive and hidden, and awaits the proper time to appear.

Shi'ites believed that the world government the Mahdi is supposed to establish will last for hundreds of years, and then the day of judgement will come.
