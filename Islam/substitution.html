Some final remarks

We need to clearly distinguish between EARTHLY penalty for criminal deeds (decided and executed by the state) and the ultimate DIVINE penalty at the last judgment for breaking the law of God.

No Christian would claim that a murderer or a defrauder who genuinely repents and accepts Jesus as his Savior from sin and Lord over his life should not be punished (here on earth) because God also forgives him.

The authority of the state was instituted by God and must judge according to justice, based on the law of the country.

Thus a Christian understanding does not make light of sins and crimes. We stand for justice in this life, within the justice system, and thus the Christian message does not encourage people to lead lawless and sinful lives since "we are forgiven anyway."

The topic of the above article was focused on the eternal punishment that we deserve but which God in his mercy and grace saves us from.

And in this eternal punishment, we are ALL guilty. Every Christian, every Muslim, everyone.

Nobody would make it to heaven based on his own merits. And even Islam acknowledges that.

Without the mercy of Allah, ALL deserve punishment and would go to Hell.

Against this background, the question that needs to be asked when comparing Christianity and Islam is this:

What is more a reflection of truly divine justice?

Or, as pointed out by certain hadiths, who punishes and tortures Jews and Christians for the sins of the Muslims. To make matters even worse, according to Muhammad there will actually be certain individuals who have not performed any good deeds that will receive a reward for every single sin they have committed! This means that Allah is not simply amoral, but immoral and unholy, a being who perverts justice and promotes wickedness and sin!
