YHWH: The Son of the Most High

According to the oldest extant Hebrew copy of Deuteronomy, which was discovered among the Dead Sea Scrolls in 1947, the Most High divided the nations according to the number of the sons of God:

The above reading is supported by the Greek versions of the Hebrew Bible, i.e., the Septuagint (LXX), which identifies these beings as angels:

Certain biblical scholars believe that this version of the Hebrew text includes YHWH among the sons of God whom the Most High allotted Israel to be his inheritance.

YHWH: The Dispossessor of the Gods

These very scholars also believe that the following Psalm speaks to this same theme:

According to critical scholarship, the God (Elohim) who stands in the congregation of God (El) to judge the other gods (elohim) is YHWH. This is confirmed elsewhere in the Psalter, where YHWH is described as the God who comes to judge the earth:

Accordingly, these critical scholars take the position that the assembly in Psalm 82 belongs to El Most High, which makes the connection to Deuteronomy 32:8-9 that much tighter. The sons of the Most High are, thus, the same divine beings that the Deuteronomist claims have been appointed to rule the nations.

The notion would therefore be that YHWH arises to condemn these gods to death in order to disinherit them from reigning over the nations. YHWH will then actively take possession of these nations in order to reign as King over all the earth.

The reason why YHWH is able to destroy these alleged divine beings is because none of them are comparable to him. YHWH is so much greater than these so-called gods that he not only subjugates them to himself, they will also be forced to worship him in light of his incomparability and transcendence over them all:

We now come full circle.

According to this interpretation of Daniel’s vision, which is articulated and defended by Orthodox Jewish scholar and Hebrew University professor Dr. Michael Segal, the Ancient of Days is a reference to El Elyon, or God Most High. Segal believes that this would then make YHWH a/the Son of the Ancient of Days.

Therefore, Daniel 7 envisions a time when the Most High God will authorize his Son YHWH, the One like the Son of Man who comes riding on the clouds, to destroy the angelic rulers of the earth in order to claim all the nations as his inheritance.

Jesus Christ: YHWH the Son of God becomes Man!
