Ps. 22:16: Ancient Support for “Pierced”

September 29, 2026
bible psalms #Beautified

In this post, I will provide some of the oldest, ancient evidence that Psalm 22:16 (17 in some versions), originally read “they have pierced my hands and feet,” in contrast to the reading found in some later Hebrew Masoretic manuscripts, “like a lion my hands and feet.” I begin with the oldest extant Hebrew copy of Psalm 22:16[17], dated between 50-100 AD.

[1] The MT says “like a lion” rather than “they have pierced.” There is a one letter difference between the two readings. This scroll reads “they have pierced.“ Nahal Hever Psalms
