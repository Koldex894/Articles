Here is an excerpt from a rabbinic source, which gives us an idea of what the rabbis thought about the sky and space above us, as well as the number of spiritual dimensions that exist. The quotations will also highlight the problem the rabbis had with the book of Ezekiel for seemingly contradicting the Torah. Just as interesting is the fact that the rabbis appeal to the book of Sirach and identify the morning star of Isaiah 14:12 as king Nebuchadnezzar. We even the source of Muhammad’s claim that there is a five-hundred-year distance between each of the firmaments, since this is precisely what the rabbis taught.
