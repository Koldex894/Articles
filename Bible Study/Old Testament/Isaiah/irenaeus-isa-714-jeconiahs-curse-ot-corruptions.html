Irenaeus, Isa. 7:14, Jeconiah’s Curse & OT Corruptions

August 22, 2025
isaiah prophecy church-history bible jesus-christ mary jeremiah #Beautified 2025

In this post I will share what St. Irenaeus wrote in respect to the prophecy of Isaiah 7:14, which speaks of the Virgin giving birth to Immanuel. Irenaeus exposes the willful mistranslation of the Hebrew into Greek by two converts named Theodotion and Aquila, and appeals to the ancient pre-Christian Greek version of the Hebrew Bible to prove that the correct translation is virgin, not young woman. Irenaeus also mentions God’s curse upon Jeconiah’s line mentioned by the prophet Jeremiah to show why Jesus could not be the biological son of Joseph, since the latter is a physical descendant of accurse Jeconiah. All emphasis will be mine.  

Chapter 21 A vindication of the prophecy in Isaiah (7:14) against the misinterpretations of Theodotion, Aquila, the Ebionites, and the Jews. Authority of the Septuagint version. Arguments in proof that Christ was born of a Virgin.
