Even More OT Proof for the Messiah’s Godhead Pt. 1

Sam Shamoun

In this article we are going to present additional proof that the Hebrew Bible portrays the Messiah as God Almighty in the flesh, while also presenting him as someone that is personally distinct from God. We will show that the God-breathed Scriptures attribute to the Messiah the very same roles and functions which God alone is said to assume and/or carry out, and therefore clearly affirm the Messiah’s divine identity.

Who Judges the World?

Yahweh God

The inspired Scriptures describe God as the Judge of all,

Who shall come to judge the world in perfect justice,

repaying everyone according to what they have earned,

on the day that he has appointed. The sacred writings also testify that Yahweh God will do so from Zion in Jerusalem, since that is the place where he will dwell and reign from forever:

The Holy Bible further claims that the reason God judges the world is because all the nations are his inheritance, his own possession, since the whole earth and everything in it belongs to him:

The nations are therefore accountable to him who made them for his glory:

The Messiah

And yet according to these same Scriptures, it is the Messiah Son of David that shall judge the nations:

The Messiah is also depicted as being the One who destroys the wicked and those who oppose him in his wrath, as he reigns from God’s right hand in Zion as the sovereign Lord of all creation:

The God-breathed revelation further testifies that the reason why the Messiah has been given the responsibility of judging the entire world is because God has assigned to him a universal kingdom:

As a result, all the nations have been given to him as his rightful inheritance:

As such, all the nations are expected to worship and serve him in the same way that they worship and serve God:
