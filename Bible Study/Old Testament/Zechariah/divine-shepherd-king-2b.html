We arrive at the final part of our examination.

The God-Man Has Arrived!

According to the inspired Christian Scriptures, the One who was to come to reign as the divine Son of David is none other than the Lord Jesus Christ!

For instance, the NT writings identify Jesus as the Child whom Isaiah said would be the great Light that reigns upon David’s throne as the Mighty God forever:

Jesus is even said to have been born in Bethlehem, in perfect fulfillment of the prophecy from Micah which spoke of an eternal Being coming to shepherd his people Israel:

Christ is further portrayed as the good Shepherd who cares and dies for his flock,

In order to make them worthy to stand in the presence of God forever and ever:

In fact, the Lord not only seeks and saves those who are lost,

He also comes to judge between his flock in order repay everyone according to what they have earned:

Amazingly, the risen Lord takes the following words of Yahweh himself,

And apply them to his own Person!

Now Christ could only do this is if he happens to be Yahweh God Almighty who became flesh (even though he is not the Father or the Holy Spirit).

To top it all off, Christ is now shepherding his people from God’s throne in the very midst of the heavenly host, which includes the cherubim!

Hence, the NT reveals to us the identity of the God-Man, the eternal One who became a human Son of David in order to perfectly fulfill all of the promises God made to the house of Israel and David their king.

Amen! Come LORD Jesus Christ, come! We confess and worship you as the God-Man, the eternally beloved Son of God and reigning Son of David, and as the divine Shepherd who sits enthroned forever and ever! Amen.
