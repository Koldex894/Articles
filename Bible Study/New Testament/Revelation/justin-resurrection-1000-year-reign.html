JUSTIN, RESURRECTION & 1,000 YEAR REIGN

March 10, 2025
resurrection revelation church-history theology prophecy christianity #Beautified 2025

In this post I will cite from Justin Martyr’s dialogue with Trypho the Jew to show how this holy martyr taught that anyone claiming to be a Christian but who denies that there will be a physical, bodily resurrection of the dead are blasphemous heretics. Justin also proclaims that there were true believers who did not hold to the rebuilding of Jerusalem and that Christ will then return to dwell there with his saints. He and a host of others, however, do believe this and further argues for the thousand-year reign of Christ being a literal, physical reign to take place on earth at Christ’s second coming. Justin then sets out to prove his position from the Scriptures. Justin further shows that God has transferred all the prophetic gifts from Israel to the Church, proving that the true followers of Christ are now the people of God, not national Israel.
