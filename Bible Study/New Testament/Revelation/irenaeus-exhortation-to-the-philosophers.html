Irenaeus’ Exhortation to the Philosophers

July 28, 2025
church-history theology trinity revelation jesus-christ bible #Beautified 2025

St. Irenaeus, in his work refuting the gnostic heretics titled Against Heresies, exhorts believers not to venture into matters that are beyond the comprehension of human beings or that have not been revealed by God in the divine Scriptures. This holy saint and martyr of Jesus Christ warns against likening the Son’s eternal, ineffable begetting to the manner in which humans beget or bring forth their human words, since the Son’s generation is beyond time and comprehension.

These statements from the former Bishop of Lyons, France, serve as a helpful reminder and warning to Christian philosophers today that they must guard against the desire to figure out the nature of God to the extent of trying to make every aspect of his Being sensible or comprehensible.

This former pupil of St. Polycarp, the Bishop of Smyrna and personal disciple of the Holy Apostles, stresses the importance of leaving such issues to God who alone perfectly comprehends his own nature and intra-Trinitarian relationships. All emphasis will be mine.     

Perfect knowledge cannot be attained in the present life: many questions must be submissively left in the hands of God.
