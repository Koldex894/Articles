Dan: The Tribe of Antichrist

March 7, 2026
christianity church-history daniel prophecy revelation satan #Beautified

There were some early Christian writers and saints who actually believed that the Antichrist was a Danite, meaning that he would arise from the tribe of Dan. Some even thought that there would be third temple built, in which the Antichrist would enter and take his seat as God.

In this post I cite two early fathers that held to this view. All emphasis will be mine.

Irenaeus
