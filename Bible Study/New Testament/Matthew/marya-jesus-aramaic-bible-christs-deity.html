MarYa Jesus: Aramaic Bible & Christ’s Deity

April 24, 2026
bible jesus god theology matthew isaiah #Beautified

The Aramaic Bible, commonly referred to as the Peshitta, employs a unique word MarYah, which some authorities believe is a compound phrase consisting of Aramaic Mar (“Lord”) and the shortened form of the Tetragrammaton or the divine name yod-heh-vav-heh, namely, Yah. If this is so then the term literally means "The Lord Yah," or "The Lord Jehovah." 

What makes this all the more interesting is that the New Testament uses this phrase for the Lord Jesus himself. This means that the translators believed that Jesus Christ is YHWH God Almighty who became a human being.
