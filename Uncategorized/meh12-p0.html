  1. Invite 10 Muslims who say,

    "I am a Muslim and I have read the Qur'an."

    (First off, you'll have a hard time finding 10 Muslims who have
    read the Qur'an in Arabic, for the vast majority of Muslims in the

    of Arabic at best. Secondly, it is important to have read the Qur'an
    in Arabic for THAT IS the Qur'an. Any translation is only the
    interpretation or meaning of the Qur'an, but not the Qur'an itself.)



    Ask these Muslims just one question:

    "What is a Muslim according to the Qur'an?"



    Please note how many dissimilar answers you may receive -

    An Ahmadiyya

    A Murji'a

    A Mu'tazilite

    A Shi'a

    A Sunni

    A Qadariyya

    A Wahabiyya

    One who makes Shahadah only

    One who makes Shahadah and believes in the sunna

    One who makes Shahadah, believes in the sunna, and is faithful to it



    In fact, there are too many sects to enumerate.
