POPE GELASIUS AND THE ASSUMPTION

June 7, 2023
mary catholicism church-history theology saints #Beautified 2023

By William Albrecht

Question: Did Pope St. Gelasius DENY the Bodily Assumption of Mary?

Answer: NO! Like St. Epiphanius, and Saint Damascene and others, he CONDEMNED THE HERETICAL TRANSITUS literature.

How do we know he AFFIRMED the Assumption? Because in his AUTHENTIC GELASIAN SACRAMENTARY, he has the sermon for the Feast Day and a dedication to Holy Mary on that Feast Day. Here is the translated portion from Latin to English: 

For the Feast of the Assumption

Gelasian Sacramentary for the Assumption of Mary on 15 August (xviii, Kalendas Septembris)
