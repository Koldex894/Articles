Responses to "It is Truth"

Chapter 15

Marine Phenomena Between Scientific Discoveries and the Qur'aanic Verses

There is very little to say about this chapter. In spite of its title, it says NOTHING about marine phenomena or scientific discoveries.

The chapter consists entirely of a round of applause for the Qur'an, as opposed to the other chapters which are composed mostly of a round of applause for the Qur'an.

The author did make one interesting statement:

This is not the mission or methodology of science. Science must be an unbiased search for the truth where only that which can be positively proven can be considered factual.

Attempting to derive science out of books that were not intended to be scientific treatises produce "junk science", as this site demonstrates.
